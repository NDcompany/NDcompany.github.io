## How our prices work

### Starting prices, set by market

We set prices separately for India and for clients abroad, so a US dollar price is not a conversion of the rupee price. The site shows rupees in India and dollars elsewhere, and the switch below changes it.

### A written quote before work starts

Your quote names the deliverables, the price and its currency. One-off projects are billed at a fixed price or by the hour, and monthly services month by month.

### Half up front for most projects

Most projects start with a 50% advance, and the balance is due before final delivery.

### No GST added

We are not registered for GST, so our prices and invoices add none.

### Monthly services end on 30 days' notice

SEO, marketing, social media and content run month to month. Paid yearly, they cost about ten months' fees.

## What changes the price

### Scope

The number of pages, products, posts or videos, and how much of the content we write rather than you supply.

### Platform and features

A CMS site, a Shopify store and a custom web application take different amounts of work, and so do payment gateways, integrations and user accounts.

### Changes outside the agreed scope

Each quote includes its revision rounds, usually two or three. Changes beyond the agreed scope are billed separately, after you approve them.

### Costs paid to others

You pay third-party costs directly: ad spend, platform subscriptions such as a Shopify plan or paid apps, domains, hosting and your payment gateway's fees, with the accounts in your name where practical. A package that includes one says so, such as the year of hosting in the Business website.

Agencies reselling our work have their own prices: see [white label pricing for agencies](https://ndcompany.in/white-label). Payment, refund and ownership rules are in our [terms of service](https://ndcompany.in/terms).

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does a website cost in India?**

With us, a website costs ₹9,999, ₹19,999 or from ₹29,000: a landing page or small brochure site, a multi-page business site on WordPress or another CMS, or an online store or web application. Outside India the same tiers start at $149, $299 and from $449.

**How much does SEO cost per month in India?**

Our SEO packages cost ₹7,999, ₹14,999 or from ₹24,999 a month in India, and $129, $239 or from $399 a month elsewhere. Paid yearly, they cost about ten months' fees.

**How much does an online store cost?**

A Shopify or WooCommerce store starts at ₹14,999, ₹29,999 or from ₹59,999 in India, and $239, $479 or from $959 elsewhere. The Shopify plan, paid apps and payment gateway fees are paid by you.

**How much does a logo cost?**

₹2,999, ₹5,999 or from ₹9,999 in India ($49, $99 or from $159 elsewhere): one concept with two revision rounds, the full set of lockups with a usage guide, or sub-brands and stationery.

**Do your prices include GST?**

We are not registered for GST, so we add none, and your invoice shows the amount you pay.

**Why are US dollar prices different from rupee prices?**

We set the prices for each market separately, from the usual rates for comparable work there, our costs and how we position our services. They are starting prices for each market, not currency conversions of each other.

**Do agencies pay the same prices?**

Agencies that resell our work under their own name have their own US dollar prices, which cover the NDA, unbranded files and reports, and calls in US hours. They are on our white label page.
