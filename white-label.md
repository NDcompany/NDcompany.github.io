# White label web development, SEO and design for agencies

We do the work and you sell it under your agency's name: websites, SEO, branding and design, Android apps and software. Nothing we deliver carries our name, we sign your NDA before you share client details, and we never contact your client.

## White label work for agencies

N&D Co. is a brand and growth agency in Barrackpore, Kolkata, West Bengal, India. Every service on this site is also available white label: you agree the brief and the price with your client, we build or run the work to your brief, and you hand it over as your own. Every quote is set in writing before work starts.

## How it works

### You send the brief

Tell us what your client needs: the service, the scope, the deadline and anything already agreed with them. Send your NDA first if you want it signed before you share details.

### We quote in writing

You get a written quote in US dollars from the agency prices below, with the scope and the delivery time, before any work starts.

### We do the work, you stay the face

We work only with you. Files, drafts and reports carry your agency's name or none, and we never contact your client or appear in their tools under our own name.

### You review, then hand over

You see every draft and decide what goes to your client. Ownership of the final deliverables named in your quote passes to you once it is paid in full.

## Your client never sees us

### Your NDA, signed

We sign your non-disclosure agreement before you share anything about your client.

### No N&D Co. name anywhere

No credit in the code, the site footer, the files or the reports, which can carry your agency's branding. We never show the work in our portfolio unless you agree in writing.

### No contact with your client

We talk to you, not to them. If you want us on a client call, we join as part of your team.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does white label mean here?**

We do the work and your agency sells it as its own. Your client deals only with you, and nothing we deliver names N&D Co.

**Will my client ever know you did the work?**

Not from us. We sign your NDA, leave our name off every file, report and site, and never contact your client. If you want us on a call with them, we join as part of your team.

**Which services can we buy white label?**

All of them: website design and development, e-commerce, SEO, digital marketing, social media, content writing, branding, logo and graphic design, video editing, and Android apps and software.

**How do agency prices work?**

The table on this page lists our starting prices for agencies in US dollars. They include the NDA, unbranded files and reports, and calls in US hours. Your final price is set in a written quote before work starts, and you charge your client whatever you choose.

**What hours do you work?**

Our office hours are Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST, and we keep part of our evening free for calls in US morning hours. Between calls we send written updates.

**Will you ever work for my client directly?**

No. While we work with you, and for 12 months after our last project for you, we do not approach or knowingly take work from a client you introduced to us. Our terms of service set this out.

**Do we pay you if our client pays us late?**

Yes. Our agreement is with your agency, so you pay our invoices on the terms in your quote, usually a 50% advance and the balance before delivery, whether or not your client has paid you yet.

**Who owns the work?**

Ownership of the final deliverables named in your quote passes to your agency once the quote is paid in full, as our terms set out. You then hand them to your client as your own.
