Kebabiyana is a restaurant, and we handled four parts of its marketing. We ran its ads, designed its [social media posts and ad creatives](https://ndcompany.in/services/graphic-design), edited its reels and [managed its social media](https://ndcompany.in/services/social-media-management).

### Social media posts and ad creatives

We designed Kebabiyana's social media posts and its ad creatives. The steamed momo creative is a square image on a mustard yellow background.

The dish name, STEAMED MOMO, runs across the middle in tall, narrow white capitals. A black plate of six steamed momos is set at an angle in front of the lettering and overlaps it. Behind the momos are two dips in small glass bowls, one green and one red, and curled red, yellow and green garnish runs along the plate. Two green leaves sit at the edges of the frame.

Only a few words go with the picture. "Special Item" sits in the top left corner and "Pure Non-Veg" in the top right, both in dark brown. A dark brown burst on the left holds the price, "ONLY 59/-", which is ₹59. On the right, a hand-drawn arrow curls down from the plate to a white "Order now!" button.

The restaurant's logo sits in the bottom left corner. It spells Kebabi in red script and the rest of the name in Bengali script, along a thin dark line with a small loop at one end, like a kebab skewer.

Apart from the logo, all the type is sans serif: tall condensed capitals for the dish name and a geometric face for everything else, regular in the corner labels and bold in the price and the button. The design uses few colours: mustard yellow, white, dark brown and black, plus the red of the logo. The rest of the colour comes from the food and the leaves.

The BBQ chicken wings creative, shown below, is also square but goes dark: a black background with orange sparks and embers rising from the bottom. "BBQ" runs across the top in large white letters with a rough, worn texture, "CHICKEN WINGS" sits under it in white capitals, and red chillies frame the edges. A white plate of glazed wings fills the middle, between a red starburst badge reading "10% DISCOUNT" on the left and a red price tag reading "PRICE 89/-" on the right.

Under the plate, one line of copy reads "Limited Time Offer: Satisfy Your Cravings with Our Unbeatable Deals!". A white "Visit Now!" button sits in the bottom left corner and the Kebabiyana logo in the bottom right. Where the momo creative asks for an order, this one leads with the discount and asks people to visit.
