## Overview

Wafflery's is a waffle shop in Bhowanipore, Kolkata. We worked on its [digital marketing](https://ndcompany.in/services/digital-marketing) and its [graphic design](https://ndcompany.in/services/graphic-design), including the square chocolate waffle creative at the top of this page.

The creative puts a chocolate waffle on a fork in the middle of the frame, in front of the Wafflery's name, with a short tagline above it and the shop's address in the bottom right corner.

## About Wafflery's

Wafflery's is listed on Google Maps at 33/1 Rammohan Dutta Road in Bhowanipore, Kolkata, the address printed on the creative. Zomato lists its cuisines as waffles, pancakes and shakes and marks the Bhowanipore shop vegetarian only, with indoor seating, takeaway and home delivery.

Zomato groups it with two more outlets under the Wafflery's name, both in Howrah: one in the Howrah Station area and one on GT Road.

Sources: [Google Maps listing](https://www.google.com/maps?cid=13787117088869005537), [Zomato page for the Bhowanipore shop](https://www.zomato.com/kolkata/wafflerys-bhawanipur), [Zomato list of Wafflery's outlets](https://www.zomato.com/kolkata/restaurants/wafflerys).

### Chocolate waffle creative

We designed a square creative around Wafflery's chocolate waffle. The waffle fills the centre of the frame, with glossy chocolate dripping down its face, and sits on a black fork that rises from the bottom edge.

Behind it, the Wafflery's name runs across the middle in rounded brown letters with a thick yellow outline, and the "a" is drawn as a small golden waffle with chocolate dripping over its top. The waffle covers the middle letters of the name.

The background is dark brown, with a warm glow behind the waffle and a large heart shape in a deeper brown. The tagline "Indulge in goodness like never before" runs across the top in thin, widely spaced serif capitals, and in the bottom right corner a map pin sits over the shop's address on Rammohan Dutta Road, near Northern Park. Apart from the name, the only text is the tagline and the address. There is no price or offer.
