# Innovation Texture case study: a brand identity for a wall texture maker

A logo and brand identity for Innovation Texture, a wall texture maker in Vadodara: a rising sun that forms the letters I and T, five palettes, lockups and mockups.

- **Client:** Innovation Texture
- **Place:** Vadodara, Gujarat
- **Services:** [Logo Design](https://ndcompany.in/services/logo-design), [Brand Identity Design](https://ndcompany.in/services/brand-identity-design)
- **Category:** [Branding](https://ndcompany.in/projects/category/branding)
- **Industry:** Wall textures
- **Palettes shown:** 5
- **Brand palette:** Brown, amber, yellow, ivory
- **Logo files:** Light, dark, duotone, round
- **Status:** In use

## Overview

N&D Co. designed the logo and brand identity for Innovation Texture, a wall texture manufacturer and designer in Vadodara, India. The logo is a rising sun that forms the letters I and T, with tall buildings, a light bulb under a small roof and stripes for the ground the business rises from. Around it we delivered four logo files, five colour palettes to choose from, stacked and horizontal lockups with their construction grids, and mockups of the logo on walls, signs and stationery.

Innovation Texture uses the identity today. Photos of it in use are covered by a non-disclosure agreement, so this page shows the presentation mockups from the brand package instead.

The work covers two of our services: [logo design](https://ndcompany.in/services/logo-design) for the mark and its lockups, and [brand identity design](https://ndcompany.in/services/brand-identity-design) for the palettes, the logo versions and the brand identity document.

## About Innovation Texture

Innovation Texture makes and designs wall textures in Vadodara, Gujarat. Its brand package describes a team of designers and manufacturers who create wall textures for residential and commercial spaces, and its goal as high-quality, customisable textures that improve how a space looks. Our concept notes name its customers as builders and residential business owners.

## What the logo was designed to say

Our concept notes give each part of the logo a job: carry the name, stand for innovation, refer to the customers and show a business on the rise. The delivered files add the practical side, a version for light and dark backgrounds.

- Carry the initials of the name, I for Innovation and T for Texture.
- Stand for innovation, with a light bulb.
- Refer to the customers: builders and residential business owners.
- Suggest a business that is growing.
- Work in black, in white and in colour, on light and dark backgrounds.

### A rising sun built from I and T

The outer shape is a rising sun: a circle that opens at the top, where tall, narrow buildings drawn in outline rise through it, and the lines of the two buildings form an I and a T. Under a small roof at their feet sits a light bulb with leaves around it, and horizontal stripes fill the bottom of the circle.

The wordmark sets Innovation Texture in a thin, geometric sans serif. It goes under the icon in the stacked lockup and beside it, on two lines, in the horizontal one, and each lockup has its construction grid.

- The logo icon: a rising sun with buildings, a bulb and stripes
- A stacked lockup: the icon above the name
- A horizontal lockup: the icon beside the name on two lines
- Construction grids for both lockups
- Logo files: light, dark, duotone and round, each 2,000 pixels square

## What does the Innovation Texture logo mean?

Every part of the Innovation Texture logo stands for something about the business: its name, its customers and where it is headed. The table follows our concept notes, part by part.

**The parts of the Innovation Texture logo and what each means**

| Part of the logo | What it stands for |
| --- | --- |
| The rising sun | Innovation Texture leading the future of wall textures; the symbol forms the I and the T |
| The tallest building | The I, the first letter of Innovation |
| The building beside it | The T, the first letter of Texture |
| The buildings together | The customers: builders and residential business owners |
| The bulb | The word Innovation |
| The bulb under the house roof | Innovating inside the home, and the business standing on top |
| The stripes at the bottom | The earth the business is rising from |

*The logo variations, from the brand identity document.*

## Colour palettes: five options and the final palette

We showed Innovation Texture five palettes, each applied to the logo, so the choice could be made on the mark itself instead of on swatches. Palette 4 is marked as custom: it keeps palette 1's amber and deep brown and pairs them with a deeper yellow and a light grey.

**The five colour palettes shown for Innovation Texture**

| Palette | Colours (HEX) | Character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Palette 1 (final) | #F6F1E9, #FFD93D, #FF9A00, #4F200D | Warm ivory, golden yellow, amber and deep brown |
| Palette 2 | #7B542F, #B6771D, #FF9D00, #FFCF71 | Browns and ochre into orange and light orange |
| Palette 3 | #FF9B00, #FFE100, #FFC900, #EBE389 | Orange and bright yellows with a pale olive |
| Palette 4 (custom) | #F5F5F5, #F9C800, #FF9A00, #4F200D | Light grey, yellow, amber and deep brown |
| Palette 5 | #CFDBD5, #E8EDDF, #F5CB5C, #242423, #333533 | Pale sage greys, a soft yellow and two charcoals |

Palette 1 is the final brand palette: warm ivory, golden yellow, amber and deep brown. It is the palette in the brand identity document, the final deliverable, and the mockups use it. The logo files also come as a duotone and a round version in a yellow and a charcoal close to palette 5's, on its pale sage (#E8EDDF), and as light and dark versions in black and white.
