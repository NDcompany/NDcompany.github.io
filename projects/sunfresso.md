### Coffee shop logo: a steaming cup inside an arc

The icon is a cup of coffee on a saucer. The cup is a solid, rounded bowl with a handle on the right and a separate band across the rim, and the saucer is one straight bar. Two S-shaped curls of steam rise from the cup. A thin arc, open at the bottom, curves over the cup and the steam, and its two ends stop level with the saucer, just above the name.

Sunfresso is set in heavy capitals with soft, rounded corners and small flared serifs, and the word runs wider than the arc above it. Coffee Shop repeats the same letterforms at a smaller size, centred under the name, so the logo says what the business sells in words as well as in the picture.

Dark brown on white suits Google's [logo guidelines](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization) for search results, which ask that a logo look how you intend on a purely white background.
