# Branding projects

Logos and brand identities for new and growing businesses: a seafood wholesaler, a wall texture maker, food brands, a pub, a hair salon and a coffee shop.

## Branding projects

Branding

Sea Seven Fish, Northeast India

### [Brand identity for Sea Seven Fish](https://ndcompany.in/projects/sea-seven-fish)

A brand identity for Sea Seven Fish, a seafood wholesaler from Northeast India: 18 logo concepts, a fish with a fin of mountain peaks, a blue palette, a seal and business cards.

See the project

Branding

Innovation Texture, Vadodara, Gujarat

### [Brand identity for Innovation Texture](https://ndcompany.in/projects/innovation-texture)

A logo and brand identity for Innovation Texture, a wall texture maker in Vadodara: a rising sun that forms the letters I and T, five palettes, lockups and mockups.

See the project

Branding

Edli Delight

### [Logo for Edli Delight](https://ndcompany.in/projects/edli-delight)

A modern logo for Edli Delight, a South Indian food chain: the name in green inside a brushed circle, beside a stack of idlis.

See the project

Koustav Kitchen

### [Logo for Koustav Kitchen](https://ndcompany.in/projects/koustav-kitchen)

A logo for Koustav Kitchen, a cloud kitchen startup that sells through Zomato: a cartoon chef with a whisk in a round badge.

See the project

North East, Jaipur
