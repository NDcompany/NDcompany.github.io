# Pio Bhai offer creative for Dream Bar, a bar and restaurant in Barrackpore, Kolkata

An offer creative for Dream Bar, a bar and restaurant in Barrackpore, Kolkata, for its Pio Bhai weekend promotion.

- **Client:** Dream Bar
- **Place:** Kolkata
- **Service:** [Graphic Design](https://ndcompany.in/services/graphic-design)
- **Category:** [Design](https://ndcompany.in/projects/category/design)
- **Industry:** Bar and restaurant
- **Area:** Barrackpore
- **Formats:** Square (1:1) and portrait
- **Offer shown:** 30% off, Saturday and Sunday, 8 to 10 PM
- **Versions shown:** 2

## Overview

We designed the Pio Bhai offer creative for Dream Bar, a bar and restaurant on Barasat Road in Barrackpore, Kolkata. It is one square graphic: the name Pio Bhai in large type, a stamp that reads "Special 30% off offer", the days and hours (Saturday and Sunday, 8:00 PM to 10:00 PM) and the bar's address, set beside a tall glass of beer.

Offer graphics like this one are part of our [graphic design](https://ndcompany.in/services/graphic-design) service. Our other [design projects](https://ndcompany.in/projects/category/design) include work for Kebabiyana and Wafflery's, two food businesses.

## About Dream Bar

Dream Bar is a bar and restaurant at 43/A Barasat Road, near Amala Cinema, in Barrackpore, Kolkata. Zomato lists it as Dream Bar & Restaurant, serving North Indian, Chinese and seafood. On Google Maps it is Dream Hotel & Restaurant cum Bar, listed as a gastropub, at the same Nona Chandanpukur address that is printed on the creative. The creative itself calls it Dream Bar & Eatery.

Sources: [Dream Bar & Restaurant on Zomato](https://www.zomato.com/kolkata/dream-bar-restaurant-barrackpore), [Dream Hotel & Restaurant cum Bar on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?cid=9979039058625670054).

### Pio Bhai offer creative

The creative is a black square. The right half is a photo of a tall glass of beer, cut off by the bottom edge, with a thick head of foam, drops of condensation on the glass and a soft yellow glow behind it. The left half holds every line of the offer, so none of the text crosses the photo.

The left column reads from the top. "Pio" in yellow and "Bhai" in white come first, in tall condensed capitals, the largest type in the creative. Under them are a one-line tagline in small yellow capitals, "Saturday & Sunday" in white and the hours, 8:00 PM to 10:00 PM, in yellow. The address comes next, in small white capitals over three lines: Nona Chandanpukur, Pradarshak, Chakraborty Para, Barrackpore, Kolkata, West Bengal 700122.

The bar's name sits at the top centre, above the glass: "Dream" in bold yellow capitals with "Bar & Eatery" in white beneath it. In the bottom left corner is a round yellow stamp with a scalloped edge and a worn, speckled print. It reads "Special" along the top and "Offer" along the bottom, with "30% off" in white on a black band that crosses it at a slant. Apart from the photo, everything is black, yellow or white.

The finished file is 2160 by 2160 pixels. Instagram shares photos at up to 1080 pixels wide, sizes larger ones down to that width and crops a photo only when its shape falls outside 1.91:1 to 3:4 ([Instagram Help Centre](https://help.instagram.com/1631821640426723)), so this square would be shared whole, at 1080 by 1080 pixels.

The same design also exists as an earlier portrait version, 1545 by 2000 pixels, from the old site's files of May 2025 and shown below. The layout is the same: Pio Bhai, the tagline, the days, the hours and the address down the left, the glass of beer down the right, the 30% off stamp in the bottom left and the Dream name above the glass. The taller frame gives the glass more height and puts more space between the lines of text.
