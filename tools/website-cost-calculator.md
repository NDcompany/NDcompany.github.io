## Frequently asked questions

**How much does a website cost?**

With us, a landing page or small site starts at ₹9,999 or $149, a business website with a content management system at ₹19,999 or $299, and an online store at ₹14,999 or $239. The calculator picks the plan that fits what you need and shows its starting price in your currency, and a written quote sets the final price.

**Is the result a quote?**

No. It shows the published starting price of the plan your project fits. The final price depends on the number of pages, the features and the content, and is set in a written quote before any work starts.

**Why does a store with custom designs cost more?**

Custom page designs, a large catalog import, discount codes and abandoned cart emails each add setup and testing work, so they move a store from our Starter plan to Growth.

**What is not included in the price?**

Each plan lists it: hosting and the domain on the smallest website plan, and the Shopify subscription, paid apps and payment gateway fees on a store. A second language and written page text are quoted separately.

**What do other agencies charge?**
