What happens to your data

Each tool works inside your browser. The invoice generator and the image to PDF converter never send what you enter or add anywhere, and the PDF is made on your device.

RankGuru asks each site you name for its page, straight from your browser, and checks it there. We never see the addresses you check or the results.

With analytics allowed in your cookie settings, Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity and PostHog show us that someone used a tool page, never what was typed into it. Clarity masks everything inside the tool, and PostHog ignores clicks there.

Frequently asked questions

Do the tools cost anything?

No. Every tool is free to use and needs no account or sign-up.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The tools are web pages that run in any current browser on a computer or a phone.

Who made these tools?

We did. N&D Co. is a brand and growth agency in Barrackpore, Kolkata.

Why does RankGuru say it cannot read a site?

A browser lets one website read another website's pages only when that site allows it (a rule called CORS), and most sites do not. RankGuru has no server to fetch pages for it, so it says so instead of showing a made-up result. Open the page, view its source (Ctrl+U in Chrome, Edge or Firefox on Windows), copy all of it and paste it into Paste HTML.

How do I save the invoice as a PDF?

Press Save as PDF. Your browser's print window opens: choose Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF) as the printer. On an iPhone or iPad, use the share button in the print options and pick Save to Files.

Do I need an account or an app?

No. The converter works in any current browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with no sign-up, no download and no app to install.
