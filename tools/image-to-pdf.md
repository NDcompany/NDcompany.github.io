## Frequently asked questions

**Are my images uploaded?**

No. Your browser reads the images and builds the PDF on your device. Nothing is sent to N&D Co. or anyone else.

**Which image types can I use?**

Any image your browser can open: JPEG, PNG, WebP and GIF everywhere, and AVIF in current browsers. iPhone HEIC photos open only in Safari; elsewhere, convert them to JPEG first or set the iPhone camera to Most Compatible.

**What does Make the file smaller do?**

It scales each image down to at most 2,480 pixels on its longest side (enough for an A4 page at 300 dots per inch) and saves it at a slightly lower quality. Turn it off to keep every image at its full size.

**Is there a limit on how many images I add?**

There is no set limit. Large photos take memory, so a phone can slow down with dozens of full-size images; turn on Make the file smaller for those.
