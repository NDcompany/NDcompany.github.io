We design logos and brand identities, run social media and ads, and make offer creatives, menus and websites for food businesses: restaurants, cafes, bars, cloud kitchens and food brands, from Kolkata to Jaipur and Bhopal.

## What a food business needs from us

Most of our food work is a mix of the same few things: a logo or identity that works on a sign and a delivery app, social media posts and reels that show the food, ads for offers and launches, and print pieces such as menus and posters. You can start with one and add the rest; each is quoted in writing before work starts.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do you work with restaurants outside Kolkata?**

Yes. Our food work includes a pub and bar in Jaipur and a restaurant in Bhopal, and we work fully remotely with businesses across India and abroad.

**Can you run our Instagram and make reels?**

Yes. Our social media plans include a monthly content calendar, designed posts and captions, reels on the Growth and Custom plans, scheduled publishing and a monthly report.

**Who pays for the ads?**

You do, directly to Meta or Google from your own ad account. Our fee covers setting up, running and reporting on the campaigns; the ad spend is never part of it.

**Do you design menus?**

Yes, as part of our graphic design work: menus, flyers and brochures as a print-ready PDF and a lighter PDF to share on WhatsApp.

**Can you design a logo for a cloud kitchen?**

Yes. We have designed logos for a cloud kitchen that sells through Zomato and for a South Indian food chain, tested at a small size, such as a delivery app listing, and on a large sign.
