# Privacy policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026 · Last updated: 28 October 2026

This privacy policy explains what personal data N&D Co. ("we", "our", "us") collects through https://ndcompany.in, including our free tools at ndcompany.in/tools, and when you hire us, and how we use, keep and share it, and your rights over it. N&D Co. is based in Barrackpore, Kolkata, West Bengal, India, and is responsible for the personal data described here.

## 1. Information we collect

We get the personal information you choose to give us, plus, if you allow it, information about how you use this website.

This website has no sign-up or accounts. If you write to us through the contact form on our homepage, we receive your first and last name, your email address, your phone number if you give it, the service you are interested in and your project description, with the date and time you sent it and the page you sent it from. If you allowed analytics cookies, it also tells us what led you to it: the first page you opened on our site in that visit, the website that linked you to it, and the last button you clicked before the form. We do not record your IP address or details of your device with it.

If you ask for a free SEO audit with the Free SEO Audit button, we receive your name, your email address, your website's address and the language you would like the audit in, with the date and time you sent it and the page you sent it from and, if you allowed analytics cookies, what led you to it, as for the contact form. We keep these requests in a database of their own, apart from contact form messages, and use them to carry out your audit, send you the report with our offer and reply to you. The form also records that you confirmed you own or manage the website, whether you ticked the box for tips and offers, and which version of this policy it linked to.

To keep automated spam out, both forms use Cloudflare Turnstile. It loads only when you start filling in a form, checks in the background that a person is using it and does not read what you type. Section 6 says what it processes.

When you email us, call us or message us on social media, we receive what you send, which can include your:

- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Business details
- Project-related information

If you hire us, we also keep the project records and the payment records described in section 5.

If you allow analytics cookies, Google Analytics records how you use the site: the pages you view and how long you stay, how you reached us (for example from a search engine or a link), your device, browser and approximate location (such as your city), and the actions we measure, such as button clicks, shares, searches on our blog and projects pages, and plays of our service videos. Google says Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.

If you allow analytics cookies, Microsoft Clarity also records how you use our pages: your clicks, taps, mouse movements and scrolling, and how each page looked on your screen. We can watch a visit back as a recording or see many visits summed up as a heatmap. Clarity masks everything you type, and on our contact and SEO audit forms and in our free tools it also masks the text the page shows, so none of it reaches Microsoft. Clarity uses your IP address to work out your approximate location.

If you allow analytics cookies, PostHog also records how you use the site: the pages you view, the links and buttons you click, how fast each page loads for you, how you reached us, your device and browser, and your approximate location, which it works out from your IP address without storing the address. It does not record what you type, it ignores clicks inside our contact and SEO audit forms and our free tools, and it does not record your visits.

Our homepage and some of our service and project pages show reviews that clients posted publicly on Google and Facebook, with the names they used there.

Our free tools at ndcompany.in/tools work inside your browser. We do not receive what you type or upload in them.

If a page of this website breaks with an error, your browser sends a report about it to Sentry, a service that collects website errors for us so we can fix them. The report holds the error message and where in our code it happened, the page address without anything after a "?", your browser and operating system, and it may include the buttons you clicked and the pages you opened shortly before. It does not include your IP address or anything you typed, and it uses no cookies. When nothing goes wrong, nothing is sent.

You do not have to give us any personal information. Without a way to reach you, though, we cannot answer you or work with you.

## 2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

- Respond to your inquiries
- Carry out the free SEO audits you ask for, and send you the report with our offer and up to two follow-ups about it
- Send occasional SEO tips and offers to people who ask for them
- Keep spam and automated abuse out of our contact and SEO audit forms
- Communicate about project details and updates
- Deliver the work you hire us for, and invoice and keep records of it
- Improve our website and service offerings, using analytics only if you allow it
- Use Google's advertising features, such as remarketing, only if you allow marketing cookies (see section 4)
- Handle your grievances, which may involve AI-assisted tools (see "Contact us" below)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), these are the legal bases we rely on under the GDPR: your consent for analytics cookies, marketing cookies, offline access and any marketing email; steps you ask us to take before a contract, and the contract itself, for inquiries, free SEO audit requests and projects; and our legitimate interests in answering messages, telling people who ask for a free SEO audit about our services in the report email and up to two follow-ups, keeping this website and its forms secure, finding and fixing errors on this website, and keeping the accounting and tax records the law requires.

## 3. Email marketing and newsletters

The email with a free SEO audit report includes an offer for our services, and we may follow up about it twice: the audit form says so before you send it. Apart from that, we send marketing emails only to people who have opted in, for example by ticking the box for tips and offers on the audit form. We will send them using Mailchimp or a self-hosted email tool, and we will name the tool in this policy before the first one goes out. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and once you unsubscribe we stop. Like any consent you give us, you can withdraw it at any time (see section 8).

## 4. Cookies and tracking technologies

Without your consent, our website sets no cookies; with it, Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity set their cookies on ndcompany.in, as below. Your browser's storage keeps your cookie choice, your light or dark theme, the currency you pick for prices and, for the open tab, how far down each page you were. If you tick "Remember my business details" in our invoice generator, it also keeps those details, and if a page's code fails to load, the tab keeps a note so the page reloads only once. To choose a currency before you pick one, the page reads your device's time zone in your browser. None of this is sent to us.

If you start filling in our contact form or our SEO audit form, Cloudflare Turnstile runs in a small frame of its own and keeps one item, cf.turnstile.u, in that frame's storage in your browser. It sets no cookies. The form cannot be protected from spam without it, so we treat it as essential and it does not wait for your consent, but nothing of it loads until you use a form.

Offline access is off until you switch it on in our cookie box (Accept switches it on too). While it is on, our service worker, a small script your browser runs for this site, keeps copies in your browser's Cache Storage: our offline page and its images, our homepage, the site's icons and web app manifest, each page you open and the site files those pages use (scripts, styles, fonts and images, never videos). Pages you have opened then work without a connection and load faster later. The service worker refreshes these copies from ndcompany.in while you are online and sends nothing to us or to anyone else. If you switch offline access off, or never switch it on, we register no service worker, and the next page you open removes any service worker and saved copies left from an earlier visit, including ones from our old website.

Google Analytics runs only after you allow analytics cookies in our cookie box. It helps us:

- Analyze website traffic and usage
- See which pages and services interest visitors, so we can improve them
- See which buttons, questions, prices and free tools people use, and which pages lead to enquiries (for a free tool, which tool and what you did with it, never what you typed into it)

If you also allow marketing cookies, Google Analytics can use Google's advertising features from your first tap, click, key press or scroll on a page. Google signals is on in our Analytics account: for people signed in to a Google account who have turned on ad personalization, it lets Google link visits across devices and add demographic and interest data to our reports. Our Analytics account is also linked to our Google Ads account, with personalized advertising on, so your visits can be used to show you our ads later (remarketing). Google can also use your visit to measure whether our ads led to an enquiry: when you send our contact form, we tell Google Analytics which service you asked about, the last button you clicked and your enquiry's reference number in our records, so we can see which pages lead to enquiries; never your name, email address, phone number or message. Without marketing consent, our site tells Google Analytics to keep these features off.

Microsoft Clarity also runs only after you allow analytics cookies. It shows us where people click, how far they scroll and where a page gets in their way, so we can fix it. It sets its own cookies on ndcompany.in, and on Microsoft's domains it can set cookies that Microsoft also uses for advertising. We let Clarity use those advertising cookies only if you also allow marketing cookies. If you switch analytics off, Clarity stops at once and deletes its cookies on ndcompany.in.

With analytics cookies allowed, our site also loads Google Tag Manager, a Google tool we use to manage the measurement tags on our site. It sets no cookies of its own. Any tag we add through it follows your choices in our cookie box, and we name it in this policy before it runs.

PostHog also runs only after you allow analytics cookies. It shows us which pages and buttons lead people to get in touch, and it keeps an ID for your browser in a cookie and in your browser's storage on ndcompany.in. If you switch analytics off, PostHog stops at once and deletes them, keeping only a note that you said no.

Our [cookies policy](https://ndcompany.in/cookies-policy) lists every cookie and storage item we use, what each one does and how long it lasts, and explains how to change your choice.

Other parties can collect information about your activity across websites through our site only if you allow marketing cookies: Google and Microsoft, as described above. Our website does not respond to browser Do Not Track signals; the cookie box is how you choose.

## 6. Data sharing and third-party access

We keep client records, project files and deliverables, and invoices in our own in-house system. We plan projects and track tasks in Notion, which can hold a client's name and project details.

We may share relevant project information with freelancers or subcontractors when we need their help to deliver the work. We will require them to agree in writing to protect client data.

When we build or run software, websites or marketing for a client, we may handle personal data that the client holds about its own customers or members. We use it only for that client's work and as our agreement with the client allows, and we do not share it with third parties.

These services also receive personal data:

### Google

Runs Google Analytics for us, only if you allow analytics cookies, and its advertising features, including our linked Google Ads account, only if you also allow marketing cookies. Google explains how it uses this data in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Google Calendar also holds the calls booked on our book a call page. When you book, our server adds the call to our Google Calendar with your name, email address and the time you pick, and Google emails you the invitation with the Google Meet link. What you write about the call comes to us by email and is not put in the invitation. Google handles the calendar under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We keep a booking like any other enquiry.

### Microsoft

Runs Microsoft Clarity for us, only if you allow analytics cookies, and keeps what it records in its Azure cloud. We use Microsoft Clarity and Microsoft Advertising to see how you use and interact with our website, through usage figures, heatmaps and recordings of visits. Microsoft collects this with first-party and third-party cookies and similar technologies, and uses it to improve websites, for fraud prevention and security, and for advertising, as the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://www.microsoft.com/privacy/privacystatement) explains.

### PostHog

Runs PostHog for us, only if you allow analytics cookies, on its US Cloud in the United States. See the [PostHog Privacy Policy](https://posthog.com/privacy).

### Cloudflare

Every request to ndcompany.in passes through Cloudflare, which delivers the website, keeps it on HTTPS and protects it from attacks, and so processes your IP address and the details of each request. If a page fails to load, your browser may also send Cloudflare a short network error report. See the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

If you use our contact form or our SEO audit form, Cloudflare also runs Turnstile, which uses your IP address and your browser's TLS fingerprint and user agent to tell people from bots, as the [Turnstile Privacy Addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/) explains, and it stores the messages and audit requests sent through them for us in two databases (Cloudflare D1).

### Resend

Emails us each message and audit request sent through our forms, from our own ndcompany.in address. See the [Resend Privacy Policy](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy).

### Sentry

Receives the error reports described in section 1. They reach it through our own address on Cloudflare, so Sentry never sees your IP address. See the [Sentry Privacy Policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/).

### GitHub

Hosts this website on GitHub Pages. Requests reach it through Cloudflare, which passes on your IP address, and GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

### Our SEO audit tool

Prepares the free SEO audits on a server we rent from Oracle Cloud in Mumbai, India. It copies each request from our Cloudflare database, checks that your email address can receive mail by looking up its domain's public mail records (no email is sent and your provider's mail server is not contacted), and checks your website against public lists of phishing and other unsafe sites. Google PageSpeed Insights tests its speed. If you name a competitor's website on the form, the tool checks that site's public pages the same way and shows its scores beside yours in your report; we do not contact that business. AI models, run on that server or by AI providers, help write the report and our offer from the public pages of your website, and never receive your name or email address. The tool stores each request and report encrypted with a key of its own, and keeps a daily encrypted backup in a private GitHub repository. The keys that unlock your details are never stored there, so GitHub cannot read them.

### Email, phone and payment providers

Email sent to our ndcompany.in address goes through ImprovMX, an email forwarding service, to our Gmail account, where the messages from our contact form arrive too. Phone and payment providers carry your calls and payments to us.

### Our business software

Holds the briefs, files, call bookings, leads, quotes and invoices for our work. Our [Tools we use](https://ndcompany.in/tools-we-use) page names it, such as Google Drive, Google Calendar, Calendly, Zoho and Microsoft Office.

We give each of these services only what it needs to do its job. We also disclose information when the law requires it.

If you raise a grievance, AI-assisted tools may also process it, as "Contact us" below explains.

The services named above may process data outside India, including in the United States. If you are in the EEA, what you send us comes to us in India, and Google says it relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework to move EEA data from its advertising and analytics products to the United States. When we pass your data to the services named above, we rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the service takes part in it, and otherwise on the data protection terms in our agreement with that service. Email us for details of these safeguards.

Our free tools at ndcompany.in/tools load nothing from other services. RankGuru asks each website you name for its page straight from your browser, so that website receives your IP address, as it would if you opened the page yourself. Share buttons, our social media links and links to other websites are plain links: those sites receive nothing from this website until you click one, and then their own privacy policies apply.

## 7. Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as we need it to:

- Complete the project or communication
- Comply with legal obligations

**Retention period:** We keep enquiries, whether they reach us through our contact form, our free SEO audit form, email, phone or social media, for 24 months after they arrive, unless you became our client. We keep project records, deliverables and invoices for 8 years after the project ends, which covers Indian tax and GST record keeping, and then delete them. You can ask us to delete your data sooner, unless the law requires us to keep it.

If you are a client and ask us to delete your project data once the project is complete, we delete it and send you a PDF confirming the deletion. We keep only what the law requires, such as accounts and tax records and, from 13 May 2027, the records of processing that the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules require us to keep for one year, and delete them when that period ends.

Google Analytics deletes the user-level and event-level data it holds for us once it is 14 months old, the retention period set in our Analytics account. Our standard reports, which show only totals, are kept. Its cookies last up to 2 years: see our [cookies policy](https://ndcompany.in/cookies-policy).

Microsoft Clarity keeps recordings of visits for 30 days. It keeps a random sample of them, and any we mark as favourites, for up to 9 months. PostHog keeps what it records for 1 year, and its cookie also lasts 1 year.

Messages sent through our contact form, and free SEO audit requests, are deleted from our databases automatically 24 months after they arrive, unless you became our client, in which case they stay with your project records. Our database provider can restore deleted data for up to 30 days, after which it is gone. We email ourselves a backup of each form's entries each month and keep only the latest one, so a deleted entry leaves our backups within a month. We delete the copies in our email inbox and our own business systems on the same schedule. Our Cloudflare database keeps a free SEO audit request in full until 30 days after it is sent, fails or is cancelled, and then only its number, dates and status; our audit tool keeps the request and its report for the 24 months. When the tool deletes a request, it destroys the request's key, which also makes its encrypted backups unreadable; the keys themselves are backed up apart, encrypted with a passphrase only we hold, and a destroyed key leaves those backups within 30 days. If you unsubscribe, our Cloudflare database and the tool keep a coded fingerprint of your email address, which cannot be turned back into the address, for as long as we send emails, so that we never send you our offers again. The tool's security logs record sign-ins, administrator actions and errors, without report contents or full email addresses, and are kept for one year.

Sentry deletes error reports 30 days after it receives them.

Copies saved for offline access stay in your browser until you switch offline access off, clear this site's data or we replace them with a new version.

As a user, you have the right to:

### Data access

Request access to your personal data, how we use it and who we have shared it with

### Data correction

Correct or update your information

### Withdraw consent

Withdraw consent at any time

### Data deletion

Request deletion of your data, unless the law requires us to keep it

### Grievance

Complain to our grievance officer (details below) and get an answer

### Nomination

Name someone who can use these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, these rights apply from 13 May 2027 (see section 11). We offer them to you now.

If you are in the EEA, the GDPR also gives you, in the cases it sets out, the right to restrict or object to how we use your data, and to receive the data you gave us in a portable format.

To use any of these rights, email us from the address you used with us, or tell us the phone number you contacted us from, so we can find your details. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we act. To withdraw consent to analytics cookies, marketing cookies or offline access, use the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page, as our [cookies policy](https://ndcompany.in/cookies-policy) explains.

**Your right to object:** if you are in the EEA, you can object at any time to any use of your data that relies on our legitimate interests, such as error reports. Email us and we will stop, unless we have compelling legitimate grounds or need the data for a legal claim.

## 10. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. Our main website is served only over HTTPS, which encrypts what passes between your browser and the site: any http:// address redirects to https://. This website has no accounts or logins. Messages sent through our contact form travel over HTTPS to our own address on ndcompany.in, are stored in our Cloudflare database and are emailed to us, so what you send us stays with us and the people and services named in section 6. No way of sending or storing information over the internet is completely secure.

Our [security policy](https://ndcompany.in/security-policy) explains how we protect our website and client data, and how to report a security problem.

## 11. Legal compliance

We handle personal data under the laws of India, where we are based:

- The Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable security practices and procedures and sensitive personal data or information) Rules, 2011, until 13 May 2027, when the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 replaces the part of the IT Act those Rules are made under
- The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025. On 13 November 2025 the government notified the Rules and the dates on which the Act's sections come into force. Both take effect in stages, and most of their duties for businesses apply from 13 May 2027.

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), we also apply the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) legal bases and rights set out in sections 2 and 8.

If you are in Bangladesh, you can use the rights Bangladesh's personal data protection law gives you in the same way, by emailing us.
