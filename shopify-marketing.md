## What a Shopify store needs from an agency

Shopify marketing is the work that brings visitors and sales to a Shopify store: search engine optimization, Google and Meta ads and email campaigns, plus the fixes to the store they depend on. A store sells from search only once it loads fast and Google can index every product page, so that is where we start. SEO then brings in visitors who are already looking for what you sell, and ads bring in sales during the months SEO takes to build. You can hire us for the store, the SEO or the ads on their own, or for all of them. Each is quoted in writing before work starts, and monthly work ends with 30 days' notice in writing.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do you do SEO for Shopify stores?**

Yes. Shopify SEO is our SEO service applied to a store: an audit, keyword research for your products and collections, fixes to titles, meta descriptions, headings, internal links and product schema, new content and a monthly report. It runs month to month on a scope set in writing. We do not promise rankings, because Google decides them.

**Can you run ads for my Shopify store?**

Yes, on Google Ads and Meta (Facebook and Instagram), in ad accounts you own. We set up conversion tracking for purchases first, then run and adjust the campaigns and send a report each month. The ad spend goes from you to Google or Meta directly and is never part of our fee.

**Who pays for Shopify, the apps and the ad spend?**

You do, directly to Shopify, the app developers, the payment provider and Google or Meta. Those costs are never part of our price; our fee covers the work.

**Do you work with stores outside India?**

Yes. We work remotely from Kolkata with businesses in India and abroad, in English, with written updates and calls arranged across time zones. Clients abroad are quoted and invoiced in their own currency and pay by PayPal, through Wise or by bank transfer.

**Should my store be on Shopify or WooCommerce?**

Shopify suits most single-brand stores that want low maintenance and accept a monthly platform fee. WooCommerce suits businesses already on WordPress or that want to control the hosting. We recommend one in writing after a call about your catalog, budget and how you ship.

**Do you work with agencies too?**

Yes. Agencies that want Shopify builds, SEO or ads under their own name can see our white label Shopify page, where the work is unbranded and we never contact your client.
