## How the remote work works

### Bengali and English

Discuss your project in whichever language you think in. Briefs, calls and feedback work in Bengali or English.

### Your business day is our business day

Dhaka is only 30 minutes ahead of IST. We work Mon to Sat, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST, which is 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM in Bangladesh.

You get a written quote and invoices in Bangladeshi taka, and you pay by bank transfer from your bank in Bangladesh: half upfront, the balance before final delivery.
