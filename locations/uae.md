## Frequently asked questions

**Do you work with businesses in Dubai and the UAE?**

Yes. We take on projects for businesses in Dubai and across the UAE, fully remotely from Kolkata. Our office day runs from 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM UAE time, so calls and feedback fit inside your working day.

**Do you build Arabic websites?**

Yes. We build bilingual Arabic and English websites. The Arabic pages read right to left in an Arabic font, a language switch moves between the two, and each language has its own address, marked up so Google shows each searcher the version in their language.

**How do we pay from the UAE?**

Our quotes and invoices are in dirhams or US dollars, whichever you prefer. You can pay by bank transfer or PayPal, or through Wise if you use it. The project starts on a 50% upfront payment, and the balance is due before final delivery.

**What do your services cost in the UAE?**

Each service page shows its starting prices in US dollars. The dirham is pegged to the US dollar at 3.6725, so a dollar price converts the same way every day. Your written quote confirms the final price and its currency before any work starts.
