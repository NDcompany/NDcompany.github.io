## Frequently asked questions

**Do you work with businesses in the Netherlands?**

Yes. We work with businesses in the Netherlands fully remotely from Kolkata, in English. Our office day covers your morning and early afternoon, so calls and same-day feedback fit inside your normal workday.

**How does the time difference with the Netherlands work?**

India is 3.5 to 4.5 hours ahead depending on the season. Our office hours, Mon to Sat 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST, cover your morning and early afternoon, so calls and same-day feedback fit inside your normal workday.

**How do we pay from the Netherlands?**

Our quotes and invoices are in euros, and you can pay by PayPal, through Wise or by bank transfer. The project starts on a 50% upfront payment; the balance is due before final delivery.

**Do you build webshops?**

Yes. E-commerce development is one of our core services: product catalog, cart, checkout and payment gateway integration, with the store admin handed over to you at launch.
