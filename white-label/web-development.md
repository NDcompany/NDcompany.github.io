# White label web development and design for agencies

We design and build websites and online stores for your clients under your agency's name, on WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce or custom code. No credit in the footer or the code, and we never contact your client.

## White label web development, the way we run it

Our white-label web development is the same work as our own website and e-commerce projects. You send the brief and your client's brand files, we design and build to them, and you hand the site over as your own. Each project is quoted in writing before work starts, with a 7-day support window after delivery.

Every other service is available white label too: see [white label for agencies](https://ndcompany.in/white-label) and [white label SEO services](https://ndcompany.in/white-label/seo).

## What you can resell

### Business websites

Multi-page sites on WordPress or another CMS, with an admin panel your client can edit, set up with the theme, plugins and editor roles they need.

### Shopify and WooCommerce stores

Catalog, variants, cart, checkout and a payment gateway for your client's market, such as Stripe or PayPal for sales abroad, tested end to end with test orders.

### Landing pages

Single pages for a campaign, launch or lead form, with one call to action and a form that reaches the right inbox.

### Custom web applications

Dashboards, booking systems and client portals, scoped and built around the process rather than adapted from a plugin.

### Design to your brief

Custom layouts for mobile and desktop from your brief and your client's brand, reviewed with you before development starts.

### SEO setup and speed

Titles, meta descriptions, headings, alt text, sitemap and robots.txt configured before launch, with speed tuning on the larger tiers.

### Launch and handover

Deployed to your client's domain, forms and links tested on the live server, and the logins and files named in your quote handed to you.

## How it works

### You send the brief

Tell us what your client needs: the service, the scope, the deadline and anything already agreed with them. Send your NDA first if you want it signed before you share details.

### We quote in writing

You get a written quote in US dollars from the agency prices below, with the scope and the delivery time, before any work starts.

### We do the work, you stay the face

We work only with you. Files, drafts and reports carry your agency's name or none, and we never contact your client or appear in their tools under our own name.

### You review, then hand over

You see every draft and decide what goes to your client. Ownership of the final deliverables named in your quote passes to you once it is paid in full.

## Your client never sees us

### Your NDA, signed

We sign your non-disclosure agreement before you share anything about your client.

### No N&D Co. name anywhere

No credit in the code, the site footer, the files or the reports, which can carry your agency's branding. We never show the work in our portfolio unless you agree in writing.

### No contact with your client

We talk to you, not to them. If you want us on a client call, we join as part of your team.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there any credit to N&D Co. on the site?**

No. There is no credit in the footer, the code comments, the admin panel or the files. The site is yours to present as your agency's work.

**Which platforms do you build on?**

WordPress and other CMSs for business sites, Shopify or WooCommerce for stores, and custom code for web applications. We recommend one in writing when the brief leaves it open.

**Who hosts the site?**

Hosting and the domain go in an account your agency or your client controls. Your quote says whether a package includes them and for how long.

**What happens after launch?**

A 7-day support window covers minor corrections and technical issues in the delivered site. Ongoing maintenance is available as a separate package, also white label.
