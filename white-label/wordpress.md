## Frequently asked questions

**Is there any credit to N&D Co. on the site?**

No. There is no credit in the footer, the theme, the code comments or the admin panel. The site is yours to present as your agency's work.

**When is WordPress the right choice?**

WordPress fits sites your client will edit often. For a landing page that has to be very fast, or a web application with logic no plugin covers, we recommend custom code instead, in writing, and our white label web development page covers that work.

**Should the store be on WooCommerce or Shopify?**

WooCommerce suits businesses already on WordPress or that want to control the hosting. Shopify suits most single-brand stores that want low maintenance and accept a monthly platform fee. We recommend one in writing after discovery.

**What happens after launch?**

A 7-day support window covers minor corrections and technical issues in the delivered site. Ongoing maintenance is available as a separate package, also white label.
