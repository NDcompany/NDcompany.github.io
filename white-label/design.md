Our white-label design is the same work as our own logo, brand identity, graphic design and full branding projects, with your agency's name on the presentation instead of ours. You send the brief, we present the concepts to you, and you show them to your client as your own. Each project is quoted in writing before work starts, including the number of revision rounds.

## What you can resell

### Logo design

Concept directions as flat black-and-white marks, refined through the agreed revision rounds, then the primary logo plus horizontal, stacked and icon-only versions in full color, single color and reversed.

### Brand identity

A color palette with print and screen values (CMYK, a Pantone reference, HEX and RGB), a heading and body typeface pairing, and supporting patterns, icon style and image direction.

### Brand guidelines

A PDF covering logo usage and clear space, color values, typography, rules to follow and avoid, and examples of the identity applied, with no mention of N&D Co.

### Stationery and social templates

Business card, letterhead and email signature designs, plus editable social media profile, post and story templates in your client's identity.

### Print and marketing graphics

Brochures, flyers, menus, posters, roll-up banners and signage, as print-ready PDF with bleed and crop marks at the size your client's printer specifies.

### Ad creatives and packaging

Static ad sets for Google Display, Meta and print in every agreed size, and labels, boxes or pouches laid out on the dieline from your client's packaging supplier.

### Editable design files

AI, EPS, SVG and PDF files with fonts outlined, plus PNG exports with transparent backgrounds, organized in a folder you hand to your client.

## How it works

### You send the brief

Tell us what your client needs: the service, the scope, the deadline and anything already agreed with them. Send your NDA first if you want it signed before you share details.

### We quote in writing

You get a written quote in US dollars from the agency prices below, with the scope and the delivery time, before any work starts.

### We do the work, you stay the face

We work only with you. Files, drafts and reports carry your agency's name or none, and we never contact your client or appear in their tools under our own name.

### You review, then hand over

You see every draft and decide what goes to your client. Ownership of the final deliverables named in your quote passes to you once it is paid in full.

## Your client never sees us

### Your NDA, signed

We sign your non-disclosure agreement before you share anything about your client.

### No N&D Co. name anywhere

No credit in the code, the site footer, the files or the reports, which can carry your agency's branding. We never show the work in our portfolio unless you agree in writing.

### No contact with your client

We talk to you, not to them. If you want us on a client call, we join as part of your team.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there any credit to N&D Co. in the designs?**

No. The concepts, presentations, guidelines and files carry no N&D Co. name or logo, so you can present them as your agency's work.

**Do you present the concepts to our client?**

No. We present them to you in a file you can show your client as your own, and we take feedback from you. If you want us on a call with your client, we join as part of your team.

**Which file formats do we get?**

Editable vector files (AI, EPS, SVG and PDF) and PNG exports with transparent backgrounds, plus print-ready PDFs with bleed for print pieces. Fonts are outlined so the files open anywhere, with notes on where your client can license the typefaces.

**How many revision rounds are included?**

Two to three on a full branding project. For other work, your written quote sets the number before we start.
