## Frequently asked questions

**Is there any credit to N&D Co. in the store?**

No. There is no credit in the theme, the footer, the code comments or the admin. We work through the access you arrange for us and can use an email address you choose, so nothing in your client's Shopify admin shows our name.

**Who pays for the Shopify plan and apps?**

Your client, directly to Shopify, the app developers and the payment provider. Those fees are never part of our price.

**Should the store be on Shopify or WooCommerce?**

Shopify suits most single-brand stores that want low maintenance and accept a monthly platform fee. WooCommerce suits businesses already on WordPress or that want to control the hosting, and our white label WordPress page covers it. We recommend one in writing after discovery.

**Can you run SEO and ads for the store after launch?**

Yes, under your agency's name. Both run month to month on a scope set in writing, ended with 30 days' notice. Your client pays the ad spend directly to Google or Meta, and we do not promise rankings or a cost per sale.

**What happens after launch?**

A 7-day support window covers minor corrections and technical issues in the delivered store. Ongoing maintenance is available as a separate package, also white label.

**Where is the team, and how do we work across time zones?**

We are in Barrackpore, Kolkata, India. We keep part of our evening for calls in US mornings and send written updates between calls.
