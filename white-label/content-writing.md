## What you can resell

### Content calendar

A monthly plan listing every piece, its target keyword or campaign goal, publish date and status, shared as a spreadsheet or in your project tool, with your agency's name or none.

### SEO blog posts

Long-form articles researched against the search results for the target query, with heading structure, internal link suggestions, meta title and meta description: 4 a month on Starter, 8 on Growth, or as your brief sets.

### Website and landing page copy

Home, about, service and campaign pages written from your brief, structured section by section for your client's developer.

### Product descriptions

A short and a long version per product from your client's product data, in one consistent format, as a spreadsheet for bulk import.

### Social media captions

Captions and hashtag sets for the posts in the calendar, written to fit the platform and the graphics.

### A style sheet per client

We read what your client has published, write a one-page style sheet for its voice and check every piece against it.

### Sources and editing

Every figure, quote and fact links to the page it came from, and every piece is proofread before it reaches you, with two revision rounds per piece on Starter.

## Frequently asked questions

**Will our client see your name anywhere?**

No. Drafts come as Google Docs or Word files with no name of ours in the text, the file or its details, and we can work under an email address you choose. If you publish through your client's site, we use the access you arrange for us.

**Do you use AI to write?**

We follow your rule, or your client's. Some agencies want no AI at all; others allow it for research or outlines. Either way, every fact is checked against its source and every piece is edited before it reaches you, and we never deliver unedited AI text.

**Do you write in US or UK English?**

Either, or Indian English. We follow your client's readers in spelling, dates and units, and note the choice in the client's style sheet.

**Can we read your writing before we buy?**

Yes. Our own blog is written the way we write for clients, and our content service page picks three posts to start with: an original study, a guide and a first-hand story. [See our writing samples](https://ndcompany.in/services/content-creation).

**Do you guarantee rankings?**

No. We write each post to a target query and study the pages already ranking, but rankings also depend on the site's authority, technical setup and competition, so we do not promise positions.

**Where is the team, and how do we work across time zones?**

We are in Barrackpore, Kolkata, India. We keep part of our evening for calls in US mornings and send written updates between calls.
