## White label PPC, the way we run it

Our white-label PPC is the same work as our own digital marketing packages, with your agency's name on the reports instead of ours. Campaigns run in ad accounts that belong to your client, and ads go live only after you approve the copy and creative. Each client runs month to month on a scope set in writing, and you can end it with 30 days' notice.

Every other service is available white label too: see [white label for agencies](https://ndcompany.in/white-label), [white label SEO services](https://ndcompany.in/white-label/seo) and [white label web development and design](https://ndcompany.in/white-label/web-development).

### Google Ads management

Search and display campaigns: keyword research, ad copy, bidding, negative keywords and ongoing optimization of the account.

Facebook and Instagram campaigns in Meta Ads Manager, with audience setup, creative briefs and A/B tests of copy and images.

### Conversion tracking

Analytics and ad platform pixels set up to record form submissions, calls, purchases or whatever counts as a lead for your client.

### Retargeting and email

Retargeting campaigns and a monthly email campaign on the Growth tier, more on Custom.

### Landing page recommendations

A review of the pages the ads send people to, with specific changes to copy, layout and forms.

### Weekly optimization

Each week we review spend, pause weak ads, test new copy and adjust bids and audiences.

### Monthly white-label reports

Spend, clicks, cost per lead, what we changed and what we plan next, unbranded or with your agency's logo, ready to forward.

## Frequently asked questions

**Who pays the ad spend?**

Your client, directly to Google or Meta from their own ad account. It is never part of our fee, so the platform's invoice shows exactly what was spent.

**Can we approve ads before they go live?**

Yes. Ads go live only after you approve the copy and creative, so you can check them with your client first.

**Will our client see your name in their ad accounts?**

No. We work through the access you arrange for us and can use an email address you choose, so nothing in your client's Google Ads or Meta accounts shows our name.

**Who owns the ad accounts?**

Your client. Campaigns run in accounts registered to their business; if the work ends, they keep the accounts, the campaign history and the audiences built.

**Do you guarantee a cost per lead?**

No. Results depend on the offer, the market and the budget, so we do not promise a cost per lead or a number of leads. Each monthly report shows the actual numbers and what we change next.
