## White label SEO, the way we run it

Our white-label SEO is the same work as our own SEO packages, with your agency's name on the deliverables instead of ours. Each client starts with an audit, then runs month to month on a scope set in writing, and you can end it with 30 days' notice.

Every other service is available white label too: see [white label for agencies](https://ndcompany.in/white-label), [white label Google Ads and PPC management](https://ndcompany.in/white-label/google-ads), [white label web development and design](https://ndcompany.in/white-label/web-development) and [white label WordPress development](https://ndcompany.in/white-label/wordpress).

## What you can resell

### SEO audits under your brand

A full crawl of your client's site for indexing problems, broken links, redirect chains, slow templates and missing metadata, delivered as a prioritized fix list in your agency's report.

### Keyword research and page mapping

The search phrases your client's customers use, grouped by intent and mapped to existing or new pages.

### On-page optimization

Titles, meta descriptions, headings, internal links, image alt text and schema markup, updated page by page.

### SEO content

New pages and blog posts written to answer each search directly: 3 posts a month on Starter, 8 on Growth, or as your brief sets.

### Local SEO

Google Business Profile setup or cleanup and consistent business details across directories. Google's rules for anyone who manages a profile for someone else apply to us too.

### Link building

Outreach for links from relevant sites and directories, plus reclaiming links the site has already earned.

### Monthly white-label reports

Rankings, organic traffic, indexing status and a log of the work done, unbranded or with your agency's logo, ready to forward.

## How it works

### You send the brief

Tell us what your client needs: the service, the scope, the deadline and anything already agreed with them. Send your NDA first if you want it signed before you share details.

### We quote in writing

You get a written quote in US dollars from the agency prices below, with the scope and the delivery time, before any work starts.

### We do the work, you stay the face

We work only with you. Files, drafts and reports carry your agency's name or none, and we never contact your client or appear in their tools under our own name.

### You review, then hand over

You see every draft and decide what goes to your client. Ownership of the final deliverables named in your quote passes to you once it is paid in full.

## Your client never sees us

### Your NDA, signed

We sign your non-disclosure agreement before you share anything about your client.

### No N&D Co. name anywhere

No credit in the code, the site footer, the files or the reports, which can carry your agency's branding. We never show the work in our portfolio unless you agree in writing.

### No contact with your client

We talk to you, not to them. If you want us on a client call, we join as part of your team.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can the reports carry our logo?**

Yes. Reports and audits come unbranded or with your agency's logo, and nothing in them names N&D Co.

**Can we resell an SEO audit on its own?**

The audit is the first step of our SEO packages rather than a separate product. You get it in the first month, with a proposed monthly scope you can take to your client.

**Will our client see your name in their Google accounts?**

No. We work through the access you arrange for us and can use an email address you choose, so nothing in your client's Search Console, Analytics or website admin shows our name.

**Do you guarantee rankings?**

No. Google controls rankings, so we do not promise them. Technical fixes are usually picked up within a few weeks, and ranking changes for competitive phrases take several months. Each monthly report shows what moved.

**How do we end a client's SEO?**

Give us 30 days' notice in writing. A month that has started is billed in full.
