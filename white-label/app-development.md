## What you can resell

### Android and iOS apps from one codebase

A React Native app built with Expo that runs on Android phones and iPhones from the same code. Separate native apps in Kotlin and Swift are available on request at extra cost, set in your quote.

### Designed screens

Wireframes and designed screens for every screen in the tier, up to 8 on Starter and up to 20 on Business, reviewed with you before development starts.

### Login and user accounts

Sign-up and login for your client's customers, with account deletion inside the app and the web page Google asks for, so the app meets both stores' rules.

### A backend in your client's own account

Firebase or Supabase set up in an account your client owns. Your client pays its charges directly, along with hosting, SMS for one-time passwords and map usage.

### Admin panel

A web admin panel to manage orders, users and content without a developer, on the Business and Custom tiers, unbranded or in your client's brand.

### Payments and push notifications

Payments through a gateway for your client's market, such as Stripe or PayPal, and push notifications to its customers, from the Business tier up.

### Store submission in your client's accounts

The listing, screenshots, App Store privacy labels and Google Play Data safety form, submitted from your client's developer accounts, with up to two rounds of changes if a store rejects the app.

### Testing, handover and a month of bug fixes

Tested against the agreed scope on Android and iOS before submission, a month of free bug fixes after launch, then an optional care plan, also white label. On full payment you get the source code named in your quote.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there any credit to N&D Co. in the app?**

No. There is no credit in the app, the code comments, the admin panel, the store listing or the files, so you can present the app as your agency's work.

**Whose developer accounts is the app published in?**

Your client's. Your client opens and owns the Google Play and Apple developer accounts, and the app is listed under your client's name, never ours or yours. You set the accounts up with your client using our step-by-step checklist, then invite us with a team role under a name and an email address on your own domain, so our name never shows in your client's accounts. The store fees are your client's, and the written quote names them.

**Can you build a template app we resell to many clients?**

No. Apple rejects apps made from a template or an app-generation service unless the provider of the app's content submits them, so every app we build is a custom app for one client, published from that client's own accounts.

**How long does a white label app take?**

A Starter app usually takes 4 to 6 weeks, and a Business app 8 to 12. A custom app is timed in your quote. Store review comes on top, and a new personal Google Play account needs 12 testers for 14 days before its first public release, so start your client's accounts early.

**Can we see an app you have built?**

Yes: CHERNOBYL, an open-source Android app our founder designed and built with React Native and Expo, the stack we use for client apps. Its code, screenshots and releases are public on our open-source page and on GitHub. We have no client app to show yet, because mobile app development is a new service for us.
