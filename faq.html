Each run saves _emails.txt, _scrappedUrls.txt, _emails.csv and _results.json, plus a debug log, _MailGrabLog.txt, in the current directory.

How does MailGrab avoid junk addresses?

MailGrab (open source)

It lowercases every address so case variants count once, drops 10 known placeholder addresses and every address at example.com, example.org, example.net and wixpress.com, and reports what it dropped.
