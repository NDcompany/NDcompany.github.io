## How every engagement runs

The same four steps apply to a logo, a website or a monthly marketing retainer.

### A short call

We ask what you are trying to achieve, who your customers are and what already exists. Most first calls take under half an hour.

### A fixed quote

You get a written scope and price before any work starts. Most projects pay half upfront and the balance before final delivery.

### Work in rounds

Most projects include two to three review rounds, and your quote states the number. You see work at each stage and correct course early.

### Handover and support

Final files, access and documentation are handed over, followed by a seven-day support window for anything that needs a fix.
