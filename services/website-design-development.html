What is Website Design & Development?

Website Design & Development at N&D Co. is a custom web design and build service that covers responsive business websites, e-commerce stores, WordPress and CMS sites, business landing pages and custom web applications. Every site is designed from scratch rather than from a template, works on phones and desktops, and has the on-page basics search engines need. The service is for businesses and independent professionals that need a site built or rebuilt. N&D Co. is based in Barrackpore, Kolkata, West Bengal, and works remotely with clients worldwide.

What changes for your business

  • A site that works on every screen

    Your site loads and reads correctly on phones and on large screens. Visitors do not have to pinch, zoom or hunt for the contact button.

  • Content you can edit yourself

    For WordPress and CMS builds, you get an admin login, so text, images and product listings can be changed without a developer.

  • Search engines can read it

    Page titles, headings, image alt text and load speed are handled during the build, so the site starts with a clean technical base for SEO.

What is included

  • Responsive website design

    Custom page layouts designed for your content, with mobile and desktop versions reviewed with you before development begins.

  • Business website development

    Hand-built front end for multi-page business sites: home, services, about, contact and any other pages in the agreed scope.

  • E-commerce websites

    Online stores with product catalog, cart, checkout and payment gateway integration. Orders and inventory are managed from the store admin.

  • WordPress and CMS builds

    Sites on WordPress or another CMS where you edit pages, posts and media from an admin panel. We set up the theme, plugins and editor roles.

  • Landing pages

    Single-page sites for a campaign, product launch or lead form, with one call to action and a form that reaches your inbox.

  • Custom web applications

    Browser-based tools such as dashboards, booking systems or client portals, scoped and built around your process rather than adapted from a plugin.

  • On-page SEO setup

    Titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, image alt text, sitemap and robots.txt are configured before launch.

  • Launch and handover

    We deploy the site to your domain, test forms and links on the live server, and hand over logins and source files. A 7-day support window follows delivery.

How we work

  1. Discovery

    A call or email exchange to learn what the site must do, who visits it and what content exists. We then send a written scope, timeline and quote.

  2. Design

    Page layouts and visual direction, shared for your review. Typical projects include two to three revision rounds.

  3. Development

    We build the approved design, connect the CMS or store back end, and add your content. You see a staging link as pages come together.

  4. Testing

    Forms, links, checkout flows and page speed are checked on phones and desktop browsers before anything goes live.

  5. Launch and support

    The site goes live on your domain. For seven days after delivery we fix minor corrections and technical issues as part of the project, and maintenance packages are available after that.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost in Kolkata?

It depends on the number of pages, whether it needs e-commerce or a CMS, and how much custom functionality is involved. We quote each project fixed-price or hourly after a scoping call, and the quote is set in writing before work starts. A 50% upfront payment starts the project and the balance is due before final delivery.

How long does it take to build a website?

A Starter site usually takes 1 to 2 weeks from kickoff to launch, a Business site 2 to 4 weeks, and a store or web application on the Custom tier 4 to 6 weeks. Delays usually come from waiting on content, so having text and images ready shortens the schedule.

What do I need to provide before you start on the website?

Your logo and any brand files, the text and images for each page, access to your domain and hosting accounts, and a list of pages or features you want. If you do not have content yet, we can add website copywriting to the project scope.

Do you build with WordPress or custom code?

Both, depending on the project. WordPress or another CMS fits sites where you will edit content often. Custom code fits landing pages that need to be fast and web applications with logic that no plugin covers. We recommend one in the scope document and explain why.

Will my website be mobile friendly?

Yes. Every site is designed mobile-first and tested on phones before launch. Responsive layout is part of the standard scope on every project.

Who owns the website after it is built?

You do. Once the final payment clears, ownership of the final deliverables named in your quote passes to you, with the rights agreed there in writing. We may show the finished site in our portfolio unless you ask us in writing not to.
