What are E-commerce Solutions?

E-commerce Solutions at N&D Co. is the design, build and launch of online stores for businesses that sell physical or digital products. The service includes store design, product catalog setup, payment gateway integration (Razorpay or another Indian gateway), shipping and tax configuration, order management, and speed and search optimization of the finished store. Stores are built on Shopify, WooCommerce or as custom applications, depending on the catalog and budget. N&D Co. is based in Barrackpore, Kolkata, and works remotely with sellers worldwide.

What changes for your business

  • Orders without the back-and-forth

    Customers pick, pay and receive confirmation on their own. You stop taking orders over chat and typing addresses by hand.

  • One place for stock and orders

    Inventory, orders and customer details sit in one admin panel, and stock levels update as sales come in.

  • A store you can run yourself

    Adding products, changing prices and running a discount are things your team does from the admin, without a developer.

What is included

  • Platform recommendation

    A short written comparison of Shopify, WooCommerce and a custom build for your catalog, budget and fulfillment setup, with our recommendation and the reasons for it.

  • Store design

    Custom home, category, product, cart and checkout pages designed for your brand, with the phone layout designed first.

  • Product catalog setup

    Categories, product pages, variants such as size and color, pricing and inventory entered from your product sheet, with images optimized for fast loading.

  • Payment gateway integration

    Razorpay, PhonePe, Paytm or another Indian gateway connected and tested for UPI, cards, net banking and wallets, plus cash on delivery if you offer it. For sales abroad, Stripe, PayPal or a gateway that covers your market.

  • Shipping and tax configuration

    Shipping zones and rates, GST settings on invoices, and connection to a courier aggregator if you use one.

  • Order and customer management

    Order statuses, email notifications, customer accounts and basic sales reports in the admin panel.

  • Speed and search optimization

    Page speed tuning, product schema markup, clean URLs and meta titles and descriptions so product pages can be indexed.

  • Launch and training

    Domain and SSL setup, test orders end to end, and a recorded walkthrough of the admin for your team.

How we work

  1. Discovery

    A call about what you sell, how many products you have, how you ship and which payment methods you need. We follow with a platform recommendation and a written quote.

  2. Design

    Page designs for the main templates, reviewed on desktop and phone. Typical projects include two to three revision rounds.

  3. Build

    Theme or custom development, catalog entry, and gateway, shipping and tax setup. You get a staging link to try the store as a customer would.

  4. Testing

    Test orders through every payment method, the refund flow, each shipping rate and every notification email, on real devices.

  5. Launch

    Domain pointed, SSL active, live gateway keys switched on and a first live order confirmed. The balance is due before launch.

  6. Support

    A 7-day post-delivery support window for corrections, then optional maintenance for updates, new features or seasonal campaign pages.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an e-commerce website cost?

It depends on the platform, catalog size and integrations, so we quote each store after a discovery call, fixed-price or hourly, and confirm the quote in writing before work begins. A 50% upfront payment starts the project and the balance is due before launch. Platform subscriptions, domain, hosting and payment gateway fees are paid by you directly to those providers and are not part of our quote.

How long does it take to build an online store?

A Starter store usually launches 2 to 3 weeks after kickoff, a Growth store 3 to 5 weeks after, and a Custom build with a large catalog, several user types or an ERP integration in 6 to 12 weeks. Catalog data arriving late is the most common cause of delay, so we ask for it early.

What do I need to provide to start?

Your product list with names, prices, variants and descriptions (a spreadsheet is fine), product photos, your GST details for invoicing, and your business bank account for the payment gateway KYC. If you already have a domain, logo or brand guidelines, share those too. We handle everything else.

Should I use Shopify, WooCommerce or a custom store?

Shopify suits most single-brand stores that want low maintenance and accept a monthly platform fee. WooCommerce suits businesses already on WordPress or that want to control the hosting. A custom build is for catalogs, pricing rules or workflows the platforms cannot express, such as a multi-vendor marketplace or a dealer portal. We recommend one in writing after discovery.

Can you integrate Indian payment gateways and cash on delivery?

Yes. We integrate Razorpay, PhonePe Payment Gateway, Paytm Payment Gateway and other Indian gateways for UPI, cards, net banking and wallets, and set up cash on delivery with any rules you need, such as a maximum order value. Gateway accounts are opened in your business name so settlements go straight to you.

Do you manage the store after launch?

Every project includes a 7-day post-delivery support window for corrections and technical issues. After that, maintenance packages are available for updates, new features and campaign pages, and our digital marketing and SEO services are available separately. You can also run the store entirely in-house; the admin is set up for that.
