What are Full Branding Solutions?

Full Branding Solutions at N&D Co. is a complete brand identity service that covers logo design, color palette, typography, brand guidelines and marketing materials such as business cards, stationery, social media templates, print collateral and packaging. Every element is designed custom for the client rather than adapted from a template. The service is for new businesses, rebrands and companies whose current materials do not match each other. N&D Co. is based in Barrackpore, Kolkata, West Bengal, and works remotely with clients worldwide.

What changes for your business

  • One look across every touchpoint

    Your website, business card, Instagram post and invoice use the same logo, colors and type. Customers recognize you before they read the name.

  • Files your team can use

    You receive the logo in every format printers and web platforms ask for, with a guideline document that says how to use each one.

  • Less time explaining the brand

    Anyone you hire later, from a printer to a social media manager, works from the guidelines instead of guessing.

What is included

  • Brand discovery

    A call about your business, customers and competitors, written up as a short brief that every design decision is checked against.

  • Logo design

    Primary logo and a compact mark for favicons and profile pictures, with two to three revision rounds. Delivered in vector and raster formats for print and screen.

  • Color and typography

    A color palette with print and screen values, and a type system with heading and body fonts that are licensed or free to use.

  • Brand guidelines

    A PDF that shows logo spacing and misuse, color codes, fonts, image style and tone of voice, so anyone producing material for you has one reference.

  • Stationery and business cards

    Business card, letterhead, envelope and email signature designs, set up as print-ready files.

  • Social media templates

    Editable post, story and cover templates in your identity, so day-to-day posting stays on brand without a designer.

  • Print and marketing collateral

    Brochures, flyers, posters, banners or signage as agreed in scope, delivered as print-ready files with bleed and color profiles set.

  • Packaging and label design

    Product labels, boxes or pouches designed on your dieline, for businesses that sell physical goods.

How we work

  1. Discovery

    A call to understand what you sell, who buys it and which brands you admire or want to avoid looking like. We send a written brief, scope and quote.

  2. Concepts

    Logo directions with color and type explorations, presented together. You pick one direction and we refine it through the agreed revision rounds.

  3. System

    The approved logo is extended into the full identity: palette, typography, graphic elements and the guideline document.

  4. Collateral

    Stationery, social templates, print pieces and packaging are designed in the new identity and sent for your review.

  5. Handover

    Final files are delivered in organized folders with the guidelines PDF. A 7-day support window covers file format requests and minor corrections.

Frequently asked questions

How much does full branding cost?

It depends on how many pieces are in scope: a logo and guidelines alone costs less than a package with stationery, social templates, print collateral and packaging. Every project is quoted fixed-price or hourly after a discovery call, and the quote is agreed before work starts. A 50% upfront payment starts the project, with the balance due before final files are released.

How long does a branding project take?

The first deliverables usually arrive in 2 to 3 weeks, and the SEO, social media and support in the package then run for 3 months. A Custom package with packaging and print pieces takes longer. Feedback speed matters, since each revision round waits on your response.

What do I need to give you before we start?

A description of your business and customers, any existing logo or materials, examples of brands you like, and your product or service list. If you do not have a tagline or brand messaging yet, we can add copywriting to the scope.

How is full branding different from a logo?

A logo is one mark. A brand identity is the system around it: colors, typefaces, layout rules, image style and the guidelines that keep them consistent. Full branding delivers the identity and the first set of materials built with it, so the logo is never used on its own.

What file formats will I receive?

Vector files (SVG, EPS, PDF) for print and scaling, and PNG and JPG exports in common sizes for web and social. Color values are provided in CMYK, RGB and hex, and fonts are listed with their license source. Everything is organized in folders with a guidelines PDF that explains usage.

Who owns the logo and brand files?

You do. Once the final payment clears, ownership of all final deliverables transfers to you. We may show the work in our portfolio unless you tell us in writing not to.
