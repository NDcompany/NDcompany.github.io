## How we work

### Brief

A call to understand the purpose of the video, where it will be shown, who is in it and what footage or brand assets already exist.

### Quote and agreement

A fixed quote listing deliverables, formats, shoot days, revision rounds and timeline. A 50% upfront payment confirms the booking.

### Pre-production

Script, storyboard or shot list, location and scheduling, all approved by you before the shoot.

### Shoot

Filming at your location or an agreed venue on the scheduled day. For editing-only projects you upload footage to a shared folder instead.

### Edit and review

A first cut for your comments, then revisions within the agreed rounds. Color, sound and graphics are finished on the approved cut.

### Delivery and support

Final exports in every agreed format once the balance is paid, with a 7-day window for minor corrections.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does video production cost?**

Every project is quoted as a fixed price after the brief, based on shoot days, crew, locations, video length, motion graphics and the number of deliverables. A 50% upfront payment confirms the booking and the balance is due before the final files are released. Editing-only projects, where you supply the footage, cost less than a shoot.

**How long does it take to produce a video?**

A reel edited from footage you supply usually takes 2 to 3 days. A scripted video with a shoot day takes 1 to 2 weeks, and a series or an animated explainer is scheduled in its quote. The timeline is written into the quote, and late approvals or footage move the delivery date.

**What do I need to provide before the shoot or edit?**

For a shoot: access to the location, the people who will appear on camera, any products, and your logo and brand files. For an edit: all footage in the highest quality you have, music preferences, brand files and a note on where the video will be published. You must hold the rights to any music or stock footage you hand over.

**Can you edit videos I have already filmed on my phone?**

Yes. Phone footage works for reels and social ads if it is steady and reasonably lit. We will tell you plainly if a clip is unusable and suggest a reshoot or a cut to graphics instead. Send the original files rather than versions compressed by messaging apps.

**How many revisions are included?**

Typical projects include 2 to 3 revision rounds, agreed in the quote. A round is one set of consolidated comments on a cut. Changes outside the agreed scope, such as a new script or extra footage, are quoted separately.

**Do you make motion graphics and animated explainers without any filming?**

Yes. Animated logos, explainer videos built from illustrations and text, and animated data for a pitch do not need a shoot. We work from your script or write one, then storyboard and animate it in your brand colors and typefaces.
