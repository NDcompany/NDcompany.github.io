What are SEO Services?

SEO Services at N&D Co. is a monthly search engine optimization service that covers technical SEO (crawling, indexing, page speed and site structure), on-page SEO (keyword research, page titles, content and internal links) and off-page SEO (link building and local listings such as Google Business Profile). The service is for businesses that want organic search traffic to their website and a report each month showing what was done and what changed. N&D Co. is based in Barrackpore, Kolkata, West Bengal, and works remotely with clients worldwide.

What is included

  • Technical SEO audit

  • Keyword research

  • On-page optimization

    Page titles, meta descriptions, headings, internal links, image alt text and schema markup updated page by page.

  • Content updates

    New pages or rewritten sections for searches you do not yet rank for, written to answer the query directly.

  • Local SEO

    Google Business Profile setup or cleanup, consistent name, address and phone details across directories, and location pages where they make sense.

  • Link building

    Outreach for links from relevant sites and directories, plus fixing or reclaiming links you have already earned.

  • Analytics and Search Console

    Google Analytics and Search Console connected and checked, so traffic and indexing data are measured from the first month.

  • Monthly reporting

    A report each month with rankings, organic traffic, indexing status and a log of the work completed.

How we work

  1. Audit

    We crawl the site, review Search Console data if you have it, and check how competitors rank. You get a written audit and a proposed monthly scope with a fixed fee.

  2. Plan

    Keyword targets are agreed and mapped to pages. Technical fixes are ordered by impact, so the first month goes to whatever blocks indexing.

  3. Fix and optimize

    Technical issues are fixed, on-page elements updated and new content published in monthly batches. Anything that needs your developer or your approval is flagged early.

  4. Build authority

    Off-page work runs alongside: directory listings, Google Business Profile, and outreach for relevant links.

  5. Report

    At the end of each month you get the work log, ranking and traffic data, and the priorities for the next month. Scope can be adjusted month to month as results come in.

Frequently asked questions

How much do SEO services cost per month?

Pricing depends on the size of the site, how competitive your market is, and how much content needs writing. After the audit we quote a fixed monthly fee, set in writing before any work starts. As with all our projects, a 50% upfront payment starts the engagement and billing is fixed-price or hourly.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Technical fixes are usually picked up by Google within a few weeks. Ranking and traffic changes for competitive phrases take several months, which is why the service is monthly rather than a one-off. We do not guarantee specific rankings, because Google controls those, but we report every month on what moved.

What do you need from me to start SEO?

Access to your website admin or to a developer who can make changes, and to Google Search Console and Analytics if they exist. We also need a short list of the products or services that matter most and the cities or areas you serve. Everything else comes out of the audit.

What is the difference between technical, on-page and off-page SEO?

Technical SEO makes sure Google can crawl, index and load your pages: speed, mobile rendering, sitemaps, redirects and structured data. On-page SEO is the content and markup on each page: titles, headings, copy and internal links. Off-page SEO is what happens elsewhere on the web: links from other sites, directory listings and your Google Business Profile. The audit shows where the biggest gaps are.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings?

No. Google decides rankings, and they move with competitors and algorithm updates, so nobody can promise a position. Our terms say this plainly. What we commit to is the agreed monthly scope of work and a report on what changed.

Do you do local SEO for Kolkata businesses?

Yes. Local SEO covers your Google Business Profile, consistent name, address and phone details across directories, and location-specific pages on your site. It is part of the off-page scope for any business that serves a physical area, in Kolkata, anywhere else in India, or anywhere else in the world.
