## Frequently asked questions

**How much do SEO services cost per month?**

Pricing depends on the size of the site, how competitive your market is, and how much content needs writing. After the audit we quote a fixed monthly fee, set in writing before any work starts. As with all our projects, a 50% upfront payment starts the engagement and billing is fixed-price or hourly.

**How long does SEO take to show results?**

Technical fixes are usually picked up by Google within a few weeks. Ranking and traffic changes for competitive phrases take several months, which is why the service is monthly rather than a one-off. We do not guarantee specific rankings, because Google controls those, but we report every month on what moved.

**Do you offer an SEO audit?**

Yes, as the first step of every SEO package. We crawl the site, review Search Console data if you have it and check how competitors rank, then send a written audit with a proposed monthly scope. If you want a first look before deciding, the free SEO audit on this site covers the basics.

**What do you need from me to start SEO?**

Access to your website admin or to a developer who can make changes, and to Google Search Console and Analytics if they exist. We also need a short list of the products or services that matter most and the cities or areas you serve. Everything else comes out of the audit.

**What is the difference between technical, on-page and off-page SEO?**

Technical SEO makes sure Google can crawl, index and load your pages: speed, mobile rendering, sitemaps, redirects and structured data. On-page SEO is the content and markup on each page: titles, headings, copy and internal links. Off-page SEO is what happens elsewhere on the web: links from other sites, directory listings and your Google Business Profile. The audit shows where the biggest gaps are.

**Can you guarantee first-page rankings?**

No. Google decides rankings, and they move with competitors and algorithm updates, so nobody can promise a position. Our terms say this plainly. What we commit to is the agreed monthly scope of work and a report on what changed.

**Do you do local SEO for Kolkata businesses?**

Yes. Local SEO covers your Google Business Profile, consistent name, address and phone details across directories, and location-specific pages on your site. It is part of the off-page scope for any business that serves a physical area, in Kolkata, anywhere else in India, or anywhere else in the world.
