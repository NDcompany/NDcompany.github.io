## What are Full Branding Solutions?

Full Branding Solutions at N&D Co. is a complete brand identity service that covers logo design, color palette, typography, brand guidelines and marketing materials such as business cards, stationery, social media templates, print collateral and packaging. ## Frequently asked questions

**How much does full branding cost?**

It depends on how many pieces are in scope: a logo and guidelines alone costs less than a package with stationery, social templates, print collateral and packaging. Every project is quoted fixed-price or hourly after a discovery call, and the quote is agreed before work starts. A 50% upfront payment starts the project, with the balance due before final files are released.

**How long does a branding project take?**

The first deliverables usually arrive in 2 to 3 weeks, and the SEO, social media and support in the package then run for 3 months. A Custom package with packaging and print pieces takes longer. Feedback speed matters, since each revision round waits on your response.

**What do I need to give you before we start?**

A description of your business and customers, any existing logo or materials, examples of brands you like, and your product or service list. If you do not have a tagline or brand messaging yet, we can add copywriting to the scope.

**How is full branding different from a logo?**

A logo is one mark. A brand identity is the system around it: colors, typefaces, layout rules, image style and the guidelines that keep them consistent. Full branding delivers the identity and the first set of materials built with it, so the logo is never used on its own.

**What file formats will I receive?**

Vector files (SVG, EPS, PDF) for print and scaling, and PNG and JPG exports in common sizes for web and social. Color values are provided in CMYK, RGB and hex, and fonts are listed with their license source. Everything is organized in folders with a guidelines PDF that explains usage.

**Who owns the logo and brand files?**

You do. Once the final payment clears, ownership of all final deliverables transfers to you. We may show the work in our portfolio unless you tell us in writing not to.
