## What is Graphic Design?

Graphic Design at N&D Co. is a custom design service for the printed and digital materials a business uses to market itself. It includes social media graphics, posters and banners, ad creatives, business cards and stationery, brochures and print collateral, and packaging and label design. It is for businesses and marketing teams that need consistent visuals without a full-time designer, and every file matches the client's existing brand identity. N&D Co. is based in Barrackpore, Kolkata, and works with businesses worldwide.

## What changes for your business

### One look across every channel

Your Instagram posts, business card and shop signage stop looking like they came from different companies. We work from one set of colors, typefaces and layout rules for everything.

### Files that work at the printer

Print pieces go out with bleed, crop marks and CMYK color, so the press gets what it needs on the first send and you are not chasing a missing margin.

### Assets your team can edit

Templates for recurring pieces come as editable source files, so a new post or a price change does not need a designer. Your team swaps text and images; the layout holds.

## What is included

### Social media graphics

Post, story and cover designs sized for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and X, as PNG or JPG at the correct pixel dimensions, plus the editable source file for each template.

### Business cards and stationery

Business cards, letterheads and envelopes with your logo, contact details and brand colors, as print-ready PDF with bleed and crop marks plus editable vector files.

### Posters, banners and standees

Event posters, roll-up banners, hoardings and shop signage at the print size your vendor specifies, as CMYK high-resolution PDF or TIFF with fonts outlined.

### Brochures and print collateral

Bi-fold and tri-fold brochures, flyers, menus, catalogs and company profiles, as a print-ready PDF plus a lighter PDF for email and WhatsApp.

### Ad creatives

Static ad sets for Google Display, Meta and print placements. Each concept is delivered in every agreed size as PNG or JPG, with the layered source file.

### Packaging and label design

Product labels, boxes, pouches and sleeves laid out on the dieline your packaging supplier provides, as vector PDF with the dieline on its own layer.

## How we work

### Brief

You tell us what the piece is for, where it will appear, its final size and the deadline, and send your logo, colors, fonts, copy and any reference designs.

### Quote and agreement

We send a fixed quote and a short agreement listing scope, timeline and revision rounds. Work starts once it is signed and the 50% advance is paid.

### Concepts

We show initial layout directions as flat mockups. You pick one, and we carry it through every remaining item in the scope.

### Revisions

You send notes in one place. Typical projects include two to three revision rounds; anything outside the agreed scope is quoted separately first.

### Final files

After the balance is paid we deliver print-ready PDFs, web-ready PNG or JPG files and the editable sources. A 7-day support window for minor corrections follows.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does graphic design cost?**

We quote each project as a fixed price based on the number of pieces, their sizes and the revision rounds included. The price is agreed before work starts and does not move unless you add scope. Payment is 50% upfront, with the balance due before the final files are released.

**How long does a graphic design project take?**

It depends on the number of pieces and how fast feedback comes back. A single piece usually takes 2 to 3 days and a collateral set 1 to 2 weeks, and we agree the delivery date in writing before work starts. Sending logo files, final copy and print sizes with the brief shortens the schedule.

**What do I need to provide before you start designing?**

Your logo in vector format if you have it, brand colors and fonts, the final text for each piece, and any photos or product images that must appear. Send brand guidelines if they exist. Without any of this, we can start with a logo design or brand identity project.

**What file formats will I receive, and who owns the designs?**

Print pieces come as PDF with bleed and crop marks, digital pieces as PNG or JPG, and everything comes with editable vector or layered source files. Ownership of the final deliverables transfers to you once the invoice is paid in full. We may show the work in our portfolio unless you ask us in writing not to.

**Do you design for print as well as digital?**

Yes. Print work is set up in CMYK at the vendor's exact size with bleed, and packaging goes on the dieline your supplier provides. Digital work is set up in RGB at each platform's pixel size. If a piece must exist in both, we build it once and export both.

**Do you use templates or stock designs?**

No. Each piece is designed from your brand assets and brief. Stock photography or illustration is used only where the brief calls for it, and we tell you before any licensed asset goes in.
