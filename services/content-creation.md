## How we work

### Brief

You tell us who your customers are, what you sell, which pages or topics matter most and what tone you want, and send brand guidelines, past content and any keyword research you have.

### Plan and agreement

We propose a monthly volume, a calendar and a fixed monthly fee, and put scope, revision rounds and the billing cycle into a short agreement. Work begins on signature and the 50% advance.

### Research

For each piece we check the search results for the target query, read what competitors publish and note the questions your buyers ask. Outlines for long pieces go to you for approval first.

### Writing and editing

Drafts are written to the approved outline, edited and checked against your style sheet, and delivered on the calendar dates with a tracked-changes copy for comments.

### Revisions and delivery

You send notes in the document and we revise within the agreed rounds. Final files come as editable documents or a spreadsheet, and ownership transfers to you on full payment.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does content creation cost per month?**

We quote a fixed monthly fee based on the number and type of pieces in your calendar. It is agreed before work starts and stays the same unless you change the volume. Payment follows our standard terms: 50% upfront, with the balance before final delivery.

**How quickly can you deliver a blog post or a landing page?**

Delivery dates are set in the content calendar at the start of each month. The first month includes setup time for the brief, style sheet and research. Rush requests outside the agreed scope are quoted separately.

**What do I need to provide before you start writing?**

A list of your website pages, any brand guidelines or past content you like, product data if we are writing descriptions, and the competitors you measure yourself against. A short call with someone who knows the business well is the most useful input.

**What format is the content delivered in, and who owns it?**

Blog posts and web copy come as editable Google Docs or Word files with headings, meta title and meta description in place. Product descriptions come as a spreadsheet for bulk upload. Ownership of the final content transfers to you once the invoice is paid in full.

**Will the blog posts rank on Google?**

We write each post to a target query, with the headings and meta tags search engines read, and study the pages already ranking so the post covers what readers expect. Rankings also depend on your domain's authority, technical setup and competition, so we do not guarantee positions.

**Can you match the tone of our existing content?**

Yes. We read what you have published, note the vocabulary, sentence length and formality, and write a one-page style sheet that every piece is checked against. Without existing content, we set the tone with you in the first month.
