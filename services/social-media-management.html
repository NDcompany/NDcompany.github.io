What is Social Media Management?

Social Media Management at N&D Co. is a monthly service that plans, creates, publishes and monitors a business's social media content. It includes a content calendar, post copy and graphics, short video edits for reels, scheduled publishing, replies to comments and direct messages, and a monthly report on reach and engagement. It is for business owners and marketing teams that do not have time to run their own pages. N&D Co. is based in Barrackpore, Kolkata, West Bengal, and works with businesses worldwide remotely.

What changes for your business

  • Your pages post on schedule

    Content goes out on the agreed days, even in the weeks when you have no time to think about it. Followers see a page that is alive.

  • One look across every post

    Every graphic, caption and reel follows the same visual rules and tone, so your feed reads as one brand rather than a mix of styles.

  • Comments get answered

    We watch comments and direct messages during working hours and reply or pass them to you within the agreed window. Questions in your inbox stop sitting unread.

What is included

  • Content calendar

    A monthly calendar showing every planned post with its platform, format and caption, shared with you for approval before the month starts.

  • Post design and copy

    Static graphics, carousels and captions written in your brand voice. Designs follow your existing brand guidelines or the ones we set with you.

  • Reels and short video edits

    Short vertical videos cut from footage you supply, with on-screen text and platform-licensed audio. Filming is quoted separately under Video Production & Editing.

  • Scheduled publishing

    Posts scheduled and published on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn or whichever platforms are in scope, at times chosen from your audience data.

  • Community management

    Replies to comments and direct messages during our working hours, Monday to Saturday, with anything sensitive or sales-related forwarded to you.

  • Hashtag and caption research

    Hashtag sets and caption angles researched per platform and refreshed as the month's results come in.

  • Monthly report

    A report on followers, reach, engagement and top posts, with a short note on what we will change next month.

How we work

  1. Discovery call

    We look at your current pages together, talk about your customers, and agree which platforms are worth the effort.

  2. Agreement and access

    A written scope and quote, signed before work begins, followed by admin access to your pages and a shared folder for your assets.

  3. First calendar

    We draft the first month of content and send it for your approval. Typical scopes include 2 to 3 revision rounds before the calendar is locked.

  4. Publishing and replies

    Approved posts go out on schedule. We check comments and messages each working day and reply within the agreed window.

  5. Monthly review

    You receive the report, we discuss it on a short call, and the next calendar is adjusted from what the numbers showed.

Frequently asked questions

How much does social media management cost per month?

We charge a fixed monthly retainer based on the number of platforms, how often you post and whether community management is included. The quote is confirmed in writing before we start, and a 50% upfront payment begins the engagement with the balance due as set out in the agreement. Any paid promotion of posts is billed by the platform to your own ad account, not through us.

How long does it take to grow followers on Instagram?

There is no reliable answer, and we do not promise follower counts or engagement rates. Growth depends on your niche, how often you post, whether you promote posts with a budget, and how good the offer behind the page is. We commit to a consistent schedule, a reply to every comment and a monthly report that shows the real trend, up or down.

What do I need to give you to get started?

Admin access to your pages, your logo and brand files, product photos or footage, and a list of the offers, dates and announcements coming up. A person on your side who can approve the calendar within a few days keeps everything on time. If you have no usable photos yet, a shoot can be quoted under our video and graphic design services.

Which social media platforms should my business be on?

Fewer than you think. Most small businesses do well on one or two platforms where their customers already spend time: Instagram and Facebook for local and consumer brands, LinkedIn for services sold to other businesses. We recommend a platform mix on the discovery call and would rather post well on two channels than badly on five.

Will you reply to comments and messages on my behalf?

Yes, during our working hours, Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST. Routine questions are answered from a FAQ sheet you approve, and anything about pricing, complaints or bookings is forwarded to you. Replies outside those hours are not part of the standard scope.

Do you create the videos and graphics, or do I supply them?

We design the graphics and edit reels from the footage and photos you supply. Filming on location, product shoots and longer videos are quoted under Video Production & Editing, and larger design jobs such as packaging or print collateral under Graphic Design.
