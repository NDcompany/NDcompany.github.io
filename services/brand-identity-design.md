## What is Brand Identity Design?

Brand Identity Design at N&D Co. is the creation of a complete visual identity for a business: a custom logo with its variations, a color palette, typography, supporting graphic elements and a brand guidelines document that explains how to use them. The service is for new businesses that need an identity from scratch and for established businesses whose current look no longer matches what they do. All designs are custom, not templates. N&D Co. is based in Barrackpore, Kolkata, and works remotely with clients worldwide.

## What is included

### Discovery and brand brief

A written summary of your business, audience, competitors and the positioning the identity must express.

### Logo design

A primary logo, a secondary or horizontal version, and a mark or icon for small spaces, each in full color, single color and reversed versions.

### Color palette

Primary and secondary colors with values for print (CMYK and a Pantone reference) and screen (HEX and RGB), plus guidance on which combinations to use.

### Typography

A heading and body typeface pairing with rules for sizes and weights, and the font files or download links needed to use them in print and on the web.

### Supporting graphic elements

Patterns, icon style and photography or illustration direction that extend the identity beyond the logo.

### Brand guidelines document

A PDF covering logo usage and clear space, color values, typography, rules to follow and avoid, and examples of the identity applied.

### Stationery and templates

Business card, letterhead and email signature designs, and social media profile and post templates in your identity.

### Source files and handover

Editable vector files (AI, SVG, PDF) and PNGs at common sizes, organized in a folder you own. Ownership transfers to you on full payment.

## How we work

### Discovery

A call about your business, your customers and what you want the brand to say. We send a brand brief and a written quote with scope and timeline.

### Research

We look at your market and direct competitors, wherever they are, then agree on a direction with a mood board before drawing anything.

### Concepts

We present logo concepts with the reasoning for each, shown in context on mockups rather than alone on a page. You pick a direction.

### Refinement

The chosen concept is developed into the full system: variations, color, type and supporting elements. Typical projects include two to three revision rounds.

### Guidelines

We write and design the brand guidelines document and prepare every file format. You review it before final delivery.

### Handover

Files and guidelines are delivered on final payment. A 7-day post-delivery support window covers small corrections and file format requests.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does brand identity design cost?**

We quote each identity project individually, fixed-price or hourly, based on how many logo variations, applications and guideline pages the scope needs. A 50% upfront payment begins the project and the balance is due before we release the final files. The tiers below are starting points, not fixed packages.

**How long does a brand identity project take?**

Essentials usually takes 1 to 2 weeks from the first call to final files, Complete 2 to 3 weeks and a Custom identity 3 to 6 weeks, depending on scope and how quickly feedback comes back at each stage. The timeline is set in the agreement before we begin.

**What do I need to provide before you start on the identity?**

A description of your business, who your customers are, and any existing material such as a current logo, website or product photos. We do the research, writing and design; you need to be available for review at each stage.

**What is the difference between a logo and a brand identity?**

A logo is one mark. A brand identity is the whole visual system around it: color palette, typography, graphic elements, imagery style and the rules for combining them. A logo on its own leaves every later design decision open; an identity settles them in advance.

**Do you use templates or stock logos?**

No. Every identity is designed custom for the business, starting from a written brief and original concepts. Templates and stock logos are ruled out because the same mark can appear on other businesses and is hard to protect.

**Will I own the logo and brand files?**

Yes. On full payment, ownership of the final deliverables transfers to you, including the editable vector files. You are free to register the mark as a trademark and to give the files to any designer, printer or developer. We may show the work in our portfolio unless you ask in writing that we do not.
