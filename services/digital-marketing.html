What is Digital Marketing & Performance Marketing?

Digital Marketing & Performance Marketing at N&D Co. is a paid-media and measurement service for businesses that want leads or sales from online channels. It includes campaign setup and management on Google Ads and Meta, social media marketing, email marketing campaigns, conversion tracking, and a monthly performance report. N&D Co. is a brand and growth agency based in Barrackpore, Kolkata, West Bengal, and works with businesses worldwide remotely. Ad spend is paid by the client directly to the advertising platform.

What changes for your business

  • One owner for every channel

    Search ads, social ads and email run from one plan with one point of contact. You stop reconciling reports from separate vendors.

  • Spend you can trace

    Conversion tracking is set up before any campaign goes live, so the monthly report shows which ads produced leads and which did not.

  • Budget moves to what works

    We pause weak ads and shift budget toward the campaigns that are producing, based on tracked numbers rather than gut feel. The reasoning is written into each report.

What is included

  • Google Ads management

    Search and display campaigns on Google Ads: keyword research, ad copy, bidding, negative keywords and ongoing optimization of the account.

  • Meta ads management

    Campaigns on Facebook and Instagram through Meta Ads Manager, including audience setup, creative briefs and A/B tests of copy and images.

  • Social media marketing

    Paid promotion of your posts, coordinated with your content calendar so ads and organic posts carry the same message.

  • Email marketing campaigns

    Newsletters and promotional emails written, designed and scheduled in your email platform, with list segmentation and a send report for each campaign.

  • Conversion tracking setup

    Analytics and ad platform pixels configured to record form submissions, calls, purchases or whatever counts as a lead for your business.

  • Landing page recommendations

    A review of the pages your ads send people to, with specific changes to copy, layout and forms. Building or redesigning the page is quoted separately.

  • Monthly performance report

    A written report each month covering spend, clicks, cost per lead, what we changed and what we plan to change next.

How we work

  1. Discovery call

    We talk through your product, margins, current marketing and what a good lead looks like for you. Phone or video call, your choice.

  2. Proposal and agreement

    You receive a written proposal listing channels, monthly scope, fees and responsibilities. Work starts once the agreement is signed and the upfront payment clears.

  3. Setup and tracking

    We set up or audit your ad accounts, install conversion tracking and build the first campaigns. Ads go live only after you approve the copy and creative.

  4. Weekly optimization

    Each week we review spend, pause weak ads, test new copy and adjust bids and audiences.

  5. Monthly report

    At the end of every month you receive the report and a short call to walk through it and agree the next month's priorities.

Frequently asked questions

How much does digital marketing cost?

We charge a monthly retainer for managing your campaigns, fixed in a written quote before work starts. Ad spend is separate: you pay Google or Meta directly from your own account, so you always see exactly what the platform charged. A 50% upfront payment begins the engagement and the balance is due as set out in the agreement. We accept UPI, bank transfer, Razorpay and PayPal.

How long does it take to see results from paid ads?

Account setup and tracking usually take the first few weeks, and the platforms need a period of spend after launch before the data means much. We do not guarantee outcomes such as a cost per lead, a number of leads or a conversion rate, because those depend on your offer, your market and your budget. We report the actual numbers every month and change course when they call for it.

What do I need to provide to get started?

Access to your existing ad accounts, website and analytics, or permission to create them in your name. Your logo and brand files, product or service details with pricing, and a person who can approve ad copy within a day or two. If you have past campaign data, share it so we do not repeat what already failed.

Should I run Google Ads or Meta ads?

Google Ads reaches people who are already searching for what you sell, so it suits services and products with clear search demand. Meta ads reach people on Facebook and Instagram by interest and behavior, which works better for visual products and for building awareness before someone searches. A mix is common, and the monthly report shows what each channel cost per lead.

Who owns the ad accounts and the data?

You do. Campaigns run inside ad accounts registered to your business, and we work on them with manager access. If the engagement ends, you keep the accounts, the campaign history and the audiences that were built.

Do you also handle SEO and organic social media?

Those are separate services. SEO Services covers search rankings and organic traffic, and Social Media Management covers planning and posting your regular content. Either can be combined with performance marketing in a single quote.
