# About N&D Co.

A brand and growth agency working with businesses worldwide, from Barrackpore, Kolkata, West Bengal, India.

## Who we are

N&D Co. is a brand and growth agency in Barrackpore, Kolkata. We design brand identities and logos, build websites, online stores and custom software, and run SEO, performance marketing, social media and video for businesses of every size in India and abroad. [Nakshatra Ranjan Saha](https://ndcompany.in/team/nakshatra-ranjan-saha) founded the agency on 8 June 2025, after more than five years of work in design and digital.

## How we work

- One team takes your project from the brief to the launch, with no hand-offs between separate agencies.
- Branding, websites, SEO, marketing and video are planned together by one team, so the parts of a project fit each other.
- Every quote is set in writing before work starts, with a timeline that runs from a few days for a logo to a few months for custom software.

## How a project runs

1. A call about what you need, then a written quote with the scope, the price, its currency and the timeline.
2. A 50% payment starts the work. You review designs and a staging version as we go, on your phone and your computer.
3. The balance is due before final delivery. Ownership of the final deliverables named in your quote passes to you once it is paid, as our [terms](https://ndcompany.in/terms) say.

Clients abroad are quoted and invoiced in their own currency and pay by PayPal, through Wise or by bank transfer, depending on the country. Our office day, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST, overlaps with the working day in the UAE and Europe, and we keep part of our evening for calls in US mornings. We do not promise rankings, traffic or sales, because no one can; we promise the work in your quote and a report on what it did.

## Results from our work

For [Bangali Oikya Manch](https://ndcompany.in/projects/bangali-oikya-manch), a community organisation, we built an applicant portal joined to a case management system. By the organisation's own count, it went from handling 5 or 6 cases a day by hand to 30 to 40 on a typical day with the platform. We designed brand identities for [Sea Seven Fish](https://ndcompany.in/projects/sea-seven-fish), a seafood wholesaler, and [Innovation Texture](https://ndcompany.in/projects/innovation-texture), a wall texture maker, and ran ads, design, reels and social media for the restaurant [Kebabiyana](https://ndcompany.in/projects/kebabiyana). Every project is on our [projects page](https://ndcompany.in/projects).
