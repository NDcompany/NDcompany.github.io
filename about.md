# About N&D Co.

A brand and growth agency in Barrackpore, Kolkata, West Bengal, India, working with businesses in India and abroad.

## Who we are

N&D Co. is a brand and growth agency in Barrackpore, Kolkata. We design brand identities and logos, build websites, online stores and custom software, and run SEO, performance marketing, social media and video for small and mid-sized businesses in India and abroad. We have worked in design and digital for more than five years.

## How we work

- One team takes your project from the brief to the launch, with no hand-offs between separate agencies.
- Branding, websites, SEO, marketing and video are planned together by one team, so the parts of a project fit each other.
- Every quote is set in writing before work starts, with a timeline that runs from a few days for a logo to a few months for custom software.

See our [starting prices](https://ndcompany.in/pricing), or [book a free 15-minute call](https://ndcompany.in/book-a-call) to talk it through.
