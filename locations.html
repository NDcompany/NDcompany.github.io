How a remote project runs

Wherever you are, a project follows the same four steps.

  1. A written quote first

    Every project is quoted fixed-price or hourly, in writing, before work starts. Scope, timeline, pricing and responsibilities go into a formal agreement.

  2. Half upfront

    A 50% upfront payment starts the project and the balance is due before final delivery. Within India you pay by UPI, bank transfer or Razorpay. From abroad we invoice in your currency, and you pay by PayPal, through Wise or by bank transfer (from Bangladesh, by bank transfer).

  3. Work over calls and email

    Briefs, calls and reviews happen online during our office hours, Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST. Projects take from a few days for a logo to a few months for custom software, depending on scope, and a typical project includes two to three revision rounds.

  4. Handover and support

    Once the final payment clears, ownership of the final deliverables named in your quote passes to you. Every project includes a 7-day support window after delivery for minor corrections and technical issues.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. We work with clients worldwide, remotely from Barrackpore, Kolkata, including businesses in the United States, the Netherlands and Bangladesh.

What if my country is not listed here?

We can still work together. We work with clients worldwide, not only in the four countries above, and the project runs the same way: calls and email during our office hours, a written quote before work starts, and an invoice in your currency, paid by PayPal, through Wise or by bank transfer.

How do you handle the time difference?

Our office hours are Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST. That is 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM in Bangladesh, and it covers the morning and early afternoon in the Netherlands. India is 9.5 to 13.5 hours ahead of the US, so we work on your feedback while you are offline, and calls happen early in the morning on the East Coast, at the end of our day.

Which languages do you work in?

English and Bengali. We are a Bengali-speaking team based in West Bengal, so briefs, calls and feedback can happen in either language, whichever you prefer.
